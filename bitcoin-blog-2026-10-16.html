Retrospective exercise for 16 October 2026

A central-bank speech is not a rate decision

Use a dated Bank of England speech to practise identifying the authority and status of a monetary-policy document.

Written and published 24 October 2026. Archive study date: 16 October 2026. This page does not claim it was available on the earlier date.

Identify the document

Original source. The Bank of England page identifies Catherine L. Mann as a Monetary Policy Committee member and dates the speech to 16 October 2026. Write “speech by one member” beside the title. That is a different document type from a committee decision or minutes; the page does not itself announce a new Bank Rate.

Write a two-column note

In the first column, record what the speech actually discusses: financial innovation, dollar dominance, and possible implications for UK monetary policy. In the second, list questions you still need answered before claiming any policy change: Was there an MPC vote? Is there a published decision? What is the effective date? Leave the second column open until you have those sources.

Apply the boundary

A Bitcoin or portfolio thesis may consider macro policy, but a speech title alone cannot establish a new rate, a regulatory rule, or a price reaction. Cite the speaker and date whenever summarising the argument.
