Cookie Policy.

This page explains how cookies, local storage, analytics tags, Promotion tags, and similar browser technologies may be used on NAKAMOTO.

Essential Storage

Some storage may support basic site behavior, such as remembering theme preference, privacy notice acknowledgement, form state, or anti-spam behavior.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics tools may help measure visits, pages viewed, engagement, approximate location, device type, browser type, and navigation paths. This helps improve content quality and usability.

Promotion Cookies

Promotion partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid activity, limit repeat ads, and personalize or contextualize ads depending on settings and applicable law.

Behavior Tools

Microsoft Clarity or similar approved tools may record interaction patterns such as clicks, scrolls, and page behavior to help identify confusing pages or usability problems.

Your Controls

You can block, delete, or restrict cookies through your browser. You can also manage ad personalization through Google and Microsoft account/ad settings where available. Blocking cookies may affect analytics accuracy or some saved preferences.
