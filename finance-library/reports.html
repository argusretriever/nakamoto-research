Report knowledge

Research reports should create durable authority

A useful report has a question, data, methodology, limitations, charts, interpretation, and action framework.

Start with a question

Reports should answer one major investor question.

Example: Are ETF flows confirming or contradicting market breadth?

  • Mistake: publishing charts without thesis.
Show methodology

Readers need to know data sources, definitions, filters, and limitations.

Example: explain whether returns are nominal or real.

  • Mistake: hiding assumptions.
Make it reusable

A report should link to tools, glossary, templates, and future updates.

Example: a quarterly report becomes an archive series.

  • Mistake: one-off content with no internal links.
