7-Day Course

Reset money without drama.

One task per day. Build a clear picture of income, bills, debt, emergency savings, subscriptions, and review habits.

Lessons

Seven days, seven actions.

Day 1

Map income and fixed bills.

Write the exact monthly income and non-negotiable bills. Link: budget worksheet.

Day 2

Find money leaks.

Review subscriptions, unused services, fees, duplicate tools, and impulse spending patterns.

Day 3

List debt and interest.

Write balances, minimum payments, rates, due dates, and what happens if income falls.

Day 4

Set an emergency fund target.

Use the calculator to estimate essential costs times months of protection.

Day 5

Calculate savings rate.

Use income and savings to create a measurable improvement target.

Day 6

Organize records.

Save bank statements, payslips, tax documents, pension notes, insurance, and major contracts.

Day 7

Schedule a weekly review.

Pick a recurring time to update numbers, check progress, and decide the next one improvement.

Education note

This is not financial, debt, tax, investment, or legal advice. Use it to organize questions and records.
