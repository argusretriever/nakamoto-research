The October MPC remit reaffirms the UK inflation target

HM Treasury reaffirmed the MPC’s symmetric 2% CPI inflation target. The remit describes the policy framework; it does not announce a new Bank Rate.

Published 30 October 2026. Original source: 28 October MPC remit letter, published 28 October 2026. The 28 October date is an archive slot; this article was not published that day.

What the letter says

The Treasury remit letter, dated 28 October, reconfirms price stability as 2% annual CPI inflation. It says the target is symmetric and applies at all times. It also confirms the MPC’s operational independence. A target is a goal for policy, not a claim that measured inflation is already 2%.

What is not in this release

A remit letter is not the Monetary Policy Committee’s next vote or minutes. It does not itself change Bank Rate or establish the path of rates. The letter discusses how shocks can temporarily move inflation away from target and why the MPC must explain its trade-offs.

Reader check

Before citing the remit, label it as a Treasury letter, note the 28 October date, and keep it separate from an MPC decision. For an actual rate vote, use the Bank’s published Monetary Policy Summary and minutes.
