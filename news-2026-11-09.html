Where to find the published October UK reserves data

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 9 November 2026.

The October UK reserves publications cover official holdings and foreign currency liquidity.

How to read the release

HM Treasury’s release describes movements in government holdings. The Bank of England provides the liquidity template and identifies the asset categories covered. Before comparing totals, check whether the rows refer to holdings at period end, changes during the month or future foreign currency commitments.
