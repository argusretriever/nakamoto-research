RetirementIncome planning11 min read

Retirement income planning is about cash flow, risk, and sequence.

The retirement question is not only how much the portfolio can earn, but whether withdrawals can survive bad timing, inflation, taxes, and behavior.

Guide mapRisksFrameworkMistakesNext
Core risks

Retirement changes the portfolio job.

During accumulation, volatility can be uncomfortable. During withdrawals, volatility can become sequence risk because selling after losses can permanently reduce recovery power.

Framework

Build an income review map.

  1. Estimate essential and flexible spending.
  2. Define cash buffer and withdrawal rules.
  3. Review bond duration, income sources, and inflation exposure.
  4. Decide which accounts fund which years.
  5. Schedule annual tax and beneficiary reviews.
Common mistakes

Yield is not the same as safety.

High yield can hide credit, duration, concentration, or payout risk. Retirement income should focus on reliability, flexibility, and plan durability.
