Mistake library

The fastest way to improve is to stop repeating expensive mistakes.

This library turns common trading errors into named patterns with triggers, symptoms, cost, and prevention rules.

Impulse

Chasing

Trigger: price moves without you. Cost: late entries near poor reward/risk. Fix: predefine entry zones and let missed trades stay missed.

Emotion

Revenge Trading

Trigger: loss plus urgency. Cost: oversized, low-quality trades. Fix: cooldown rule after emotional losses.

Risk

Over-Sizing

Trigger: confidence or desperation. Cost: normal variance becomes account damage. Fix: fixed risk percent and max daily loss.

Discipline

Moving Stops

Trigger: refusal to accept invalidation. Cost: small planned loss becomes large unplanned loss. Fix: stop movement only if defined before entry.

Context

Trading Patterns Alone

Trigger: memorized setup. Cost: entries against higher-timeframe context. Fix: require market condition before setup.

Process

No Review Loop

Trigger: wanting the next trade more than the lesson. Cost: repeated errors. Fix: weekly tag review and screenshot archive.
