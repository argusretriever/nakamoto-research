Daily Check

The five ETF questions that matter.

Flow

Did shares expand or contract?

Flow data can show demand pressure, but it needs context: price, volume, creation baskets, and market-wide risk appetite.

AUM

Is asset growth coming from price or flows?

A rising AUM number may reflect Bitcoin price appreciation rather than new money.

Fee

What does the sponsor charge?

Fees affect long-term ownership costs and can change how investors compare wrappers.

Custody

Who controls the underlying Bitcoin?

Custodian, cold-storage policy, insurance language, and legal structure matter.
