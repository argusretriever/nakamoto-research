Buyer Guide

How to choose a funded trading account.

Do not start with advertised account size. Start with the rulebook, your journaled edge, and the cost of failing an evaluation.

Steps

Use this order.

1

Prove your process first.

Have a journaled setup, risk rule, sample size, and mistake review before paying for an evaluation.

2

Model drawdown rules.

Daily, total, trailing, and static drawdowns change the real difficulty of the account.

3

Read payout rules.

Minimum trading days, consistency rules, profit split, verification, and restrictions matter.

4

Check prohibited strategies.

News trading, copying, hedging, latency, weekend holding, and platform rules can cause failure.
