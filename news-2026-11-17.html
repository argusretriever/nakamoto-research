UK unemployment fell as vacancies and payrolls declined

Published 17 November 2026

UK unemployment was 4.6% in July to September. Vacancies fell to 690,000 in August to October, and provisional October payrolls fell by 34,000 on the month.

Three measures, three reference periods

The Labour Force Survey estimated employment at 75.4% in July to September. The ONS said differences between the groups entering and leaving the survey partly explain the quarterly changes. The vacancy estimate covered August to October and was down by 22,000 from May to July. The payroll figure measures employees in administrative records and is subject to revision.

Regular pay growth slowed

Regular earnings in Great Britain rose 3.5% on the year in July to September, down from 3.7% in the previous three-month period. Adjusted for CPIH inflation, regular earnings rose 0.2% on the year.

The productivity release uses a new headline method

UK output per hour rose 2.5% from a year earlier in July to September. The ONS now uses its component approach as the headline measure and has ended the distinction between flash and overview estimates. Use the historical series in this release when comparing quarters.
