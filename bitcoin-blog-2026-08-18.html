Separate a cash buffer from a market thesis

Give near-term spending its own worksheet.

Archive slot: 18 August 2026
Published 13 September 2026. This educational retrospective fills an archive gap. It is not a report of market conditions on the archive date.

Source foundation

Emergency savings provide a reserve for unplanned expenses or income disruption. CFPB: building an emergency fund.

A worked example

A fictional household lists 900 units for housing, 300 for food, and 200 for essential transport each month. The total is 1,400. Three months of those listed items is 4,200, but omitted bills would change the figure. This is a budgeting exercise, not a universal savings target.

Original hypothetical example. Amounts, people, events, and scenarios above are invented for learning; they are not historical observations or forecasts.

What to take from it

A persuasive investment story does not answer a household cash-flow question.
