Blue Book 2026: separate publication access from the data release

Retrospective archive slot: 2026-10-31. Written 3 November 2026. Published 3 November 2026.

Original source: Office for National Statistics. 30 October 2026. Verified 3 November 2026.

What the source establishes

The ONS published its 2026 Blue Book on 30 October. A notice says its content links became available at 10:35am after a technical problem, while the data had been available at 9:30am. The collection covers national and sector accounts, industrial analysis and environmental accounts.

What it does not establish

A delayed link is not evidence that the underlying data changed. The publication year also does not mean every table measures activity in that year.

Read the evidence carefully

Choose one household or corporate account table. Record its series name, reference period, units, download date and edition. Compare it with the same series in an older edition, keeping the reference period fixed.

Keep the publisher’s wording separate from your own inference. Check later corrections before reusing a figure.
