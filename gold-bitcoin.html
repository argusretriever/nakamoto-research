Two scarcity stories, very different trade-offs.

Questions worth asking.

Custody

Who controls it?

Gold custody is physical or institutional; Bitcoin custody is key management or trusted custody.

Liquidity

How easily can it move?

Gold has deep markets but physical friction. Bitcoin moves digitally but has network and platform risks.

Volatility

How stressful is the path?

Bitcoin has newer-market volatility. Gold has longer history but still cycles.
