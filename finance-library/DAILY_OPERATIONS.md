# NAKAMOTO Finance Library Daily Operations

Use this checklist every publishing day. Add fresh work without removing older NAKAMOTO Finance Library content.

## Daily Publishing

1. Publish 10 fresh updates every day.
2. Add a headline, short summary, source link, why it matters, and investor takeaway to each update.
3. Update the Daily Briefing section on the homepage and News page.
4. Make today's headlines visible in News, Market Pulse, Search, Archives, and Newsletters.
5. Check trusted source links: BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve, Treasury, SEC EDGAR, FRED, company filings, ETF issuers, and Investor.gov.
6. Refresh Market Pulse: valuation, rates, breadth, earnings, risk, liquidity, and action note.
7. Add or improve one evergreen asset: guide, glossary entry, academy lesson, checklist, calculator note, or portfolio resource.
8. Add related links from every new item to hubs, tools, glossary terms, reports, archives, and learning paths.
9. Prepare newsletter output: daily brief, weekly recap, or curated reading path.
10. Run quality checks: links, buttons, mobile layout, empty cards, accessibility, source visibility, and useful section content.
11. Track workflow status: planned, drafted, reviewed, published, updated, archived, refreshed.

## Preservation Rules

1. Preserve old content: do not remove older articles, guides, reports, tools, pages, newsletters, or archives.
2. Keep older content discoverable through archives, search, categories, topics, related links, author pages, and internal links.
3. Refresh old content with update notes, source links, context, and related resources instead of hiding it.
4. Keep dated market notes as historical records while showing newer updates above them.
