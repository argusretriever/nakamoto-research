Comparison Framework

The rulebook decides whether a trader has a realistic chance.

Cost

Evaluation and reset fees.

Compare challenge fees, retake fees, activation fees, refund rules, and whether costs fit your expected edge.

Drawdown

Daily, total, trailing, static.

Drawdown rules can decide failure faster than strategy quality. Model the rule before paying.

Payout

Withdrawal conditions.

Compare minimum trading days, profit split, payout frequency, consistency rules, and verification requirements.

Behavior

Prohibited strategies.

Check news trading, copy trading, latency, hedging, scaling, holding over weekends, and platform restrictions.

Product Table

Funded-account decision table.

Best for

Journaled traders.

Traders with a defined setup, sample size, position sizing, and rule discipline.

Key risks

Rule breach and fee loss.

Evaluation costs, drawdown breaches, payout restrictions, platform limits, and emotional pressure.

Fees to verify

Challenge, reset, activation.

Check evaluation fees, retakes, refunds, activation fees, data fees, and payout conditions.

Records needed

Trading and payout trail.

Keep rules, invoices, trade exports, platform statements, payouts, tax records, and journal notes.

Avoid if

No proven process.

A funded account is not a shortcut for traders without risk control, review habits, or emotional discipline.

Terms

Verify official rules.

Confirm drawdown, consistency, news, copy trading, payout, and prohibited-strategy rules before paying.
