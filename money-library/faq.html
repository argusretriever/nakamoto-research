FAQ

Questions users ask before trusting a finance product.

Answers for first-time visitors, returning users, subscribers, and future customers.

Is NAKAMOTO Money Library financial advice?

No. NAKAMOTO Money Library is an educational product concept with calculators and planning content. Production pages should include reviewed sources and professional disclaimers where needed.

Do the calculators store my data?

In this static build, calculators run in the browser and do not submit data to a server. A production app should explain storage, deletion, and privacy controls clearly.

Why are there country pages?

Money rules differ by country. Local hubs help users find relevant savings, tax, retirement, and debt context.

How does NAKAMOTO Money Library make money?

Potential revenue paths include templates, Pro calculators, education courses, ethical comparison pages, memberships, and employer wellness products.

What should I do first?

Use the Start Here quiz, then follow the recommended article, tool, or challenge.

Education and risk note

NAKAMOTO content is educational only and is not financial, investment, tax, legal, trading, wallet-security, or accounting advice. Bitcoin, crypto, trading, investing, and personal finance decisions involve risk. Verify primary sources and consider qualified professional advice before acting.
