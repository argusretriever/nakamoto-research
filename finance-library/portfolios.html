Portfolio knowledge

Portfolio building is a rules problem

A portfolio should connect goals, risk capacity, allocation, holdings, taxes, and behavior into one written policy.

Define each holding job

Every fund or security should have a role: growth, income, stability, liquidity, inflation hedge, or satellite exposure.

Example: broad equity ETF for growth, short-term bond fund for stability.

  • Mistake: adding positions without a role.
Use rebalancing bands

Bands reduce emotional trading by defining when maintenance is needed.

Example: rebalance when a target drifts 5 percentage points.

  • Mistake: rebalancing constantly without tax awareness.
Control concentration risk

A portfolio can be diversified by count but concentrated by exposure.

Example: several funds may all hold the same mega-cap stocks.

  • Mistake: confusing more tickers with more diversification.
