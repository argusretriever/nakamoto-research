Bailey connected regional productivity differences with the UK growth outlook

Published 20 November 2026

The Bank of England Governor’s 18 November speech discussed monetary policy and differences across regional economies. This is a speech review published on 20 November.

Separate the speech from the policy decision

Andrew Bailey explained the MPC’s earlier decision to raise Bank Rate to 4%. His remarks then considered regional productivity and labour-market differences and the role of local strengths in raising living standards.

Keep comparisons on a consistent basis

When following a regional comparison, record the measure, reference year and geography. Output per worker, output per hour and household income provide different views of economic performance.

Follow the original policy documents

Use the dated speech for the Governor’s argument and the MPC summary and minutes for the committee’s recorded decision. Label projections and conditional assessments with their horizon and assumptions.
