Debt-to-income is a ratio with limits

A practical research exercise assigned to the 11 October 2026 archive slot. Written and published on 15 October 2026; it does not describe a news event on the earlier date.

Published 15 October 2026. Archive study date: 11 October 2026.

Start with the source

The CFPB toolkit provides separate tools for cash flow, debt, and financial goals. Read the CFPB Your Money, Your Goals toolkit before using this exercise. The source explains the general process; check its date and any product-specific instructions before acting.

Work through the decision

Divide a fictional monthly debt payment by gross monthly income. Then list what the ratio omits, including rent, food, and irregular costs.

Write down what you know, what remains unknown, and what evidence would change the plan. Use hypothetical figures or a blank template if you are sharing the exercise with someone else.

Check the boundary

This practice note is educational. It does not establish a live market price, a guaranteed outcome, or a personal recommendation. For a real decision, verify current terms and seek qualified advice where appropriate.
