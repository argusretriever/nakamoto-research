August consumer credit borrowing increased to £2.5 billion

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-29. Original source published on this earlier date.

The Bank of England reported net consumer credit borrowing of £2.5 billion in August 2026, up from £2.1 billion in July.

What the release reported

Credit-card net borrowing was £1.2 billion and other consumer credit contributed £1.3 billion. The total exceeded the preceding six-month average of £1.9 billion. Net borrowing flows take repayments into account.

Mortgage approvals moved differently

Net house-purchase approvals fell to 54,900. The effective interest rate on newly drawn mortgages was 4.60%. Approvals, completed borrowing and interest rates measure different stages of the lending process. Keep borrower coverage and seasonal adjustment consistent when comparing series.
