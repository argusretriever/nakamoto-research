Retrospective source review · 22 October 2026

ONS business survey reports turnover changes and fuel-price concern

A dated original-source development, reviewed after its release. This article was written and published on 24 October 2026; it was not live on 22 October.

Original source date: 22 October 2026. Publisher: Office for National Statistics. Document type: statistics in development.

What the original source says

In the ONS Business Insights and Conditions Survey, 17% of trading businesses reported increased turnover in September from the previous month. In early October, 72% of businesses reported concern about fuel prices.

Read the original Office for National Statistics statistics in development for the full wording, context and any subsequent correction.

What this does not establish

The bulletin is labelled statistics in development and reports survey responses. These percentages do not measure total national turnover or actual future fuel-price changes.

Reader check

Keep the survey question, response group and collection window visible when quoting a percentage.
