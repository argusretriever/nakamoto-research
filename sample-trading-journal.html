No signals

This is trading education only. It does not provide entries, exits, trade recommendations, or profit promises.

Journal Fields

Measure process before outcome.

Setup

Setup quality.

What condition was present, what invalidates it, and was the trade part of the plan?

Risk

Position size.

Planned loss, maximum daily loss, drawdown status, and whether the trade respects account limits.

Mind

State check.

Fatigue, FOMO, revenge trading, boredom, overconfidence, and whether a break is needed.

Rules

Rule audit.

Entry reason, exit reason, stop movement, scaling, hesitation, and rule breaks.

Review

After-action note.

What was controlled, what was not, what repeated, and what changes tomorrow?

Score

Process score.

Grade the trade by preparation, execution, risk control, patience, and review quality, not only P&L.
