Retrospective exercise for 19 October 2026

Prepare a release note before the data arrive

A scheduled release is a question on the calendar. Its results belong in a later, timestamped note.

Written and published 24 October 2026. Archive study date: 19 October 2026. This page does not claim it was available on the earlier date.

Set the status

Original source. The ONS release page records the UK Labour Market: October 2026 publication time as 20 October at 07:00 London time. In a pre-release worksheet, write the release title, scheduled time, and variables you intend to inspect. Do not write a result in the worksheet before the source publishes it.

Pre-commit the comparison

List the intended reference periods: survey employment and unemployment estimates, HMRC payroll data, vacancies, and earnings may have different windows and revision rules. Record what would count as a meaningful change in your research question before seeing a headline. This reduces the temptation to select only a surprising number afterwards.

Close the worksheet honestly

This page was published retrospectively on 24 October. It demonstrates a preparation method; it is not a claim that NAKAMOTO posted a live preview on 19 October. The release is now available, so a current reader should open the published bulletin.
