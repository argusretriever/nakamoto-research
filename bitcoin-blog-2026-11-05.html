Separate the policy decision, votes and forecast

Published 5 November 2026

Create a three-row evidence log for a central bank announcement.

Fill in the log

  1. Decision: record the policy rate and whether it changed.
  2. Votes: record the split and what each group supported.
  3. Forecast: record the horizon and the assumptions alongside the projection.

Leave fields blank until the official documents are available. Use the evidence worksheet to record the exact source and check time.
