Lombardelli examines weaker hiring in the UK labour market

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 10 November 2026.

Bank of England Deputy Governor Clare Lombardelli described a labour market cooling mainly through weaker hiring.

How to read the release

Her speech links weak hiring to a longer-running decline in job mobility and discusses how that can affect young workers, productivity and wage pressures. Treat the argument as the speaker’s interpretation and use the cited data to examine each link separately.
