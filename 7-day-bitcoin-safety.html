7-Day Course

Bitcoin safety before Bitcoin size.

Learn the custody basics slowly: wallets, seed phrases, exchange security, small tests, scam checks, and records.

Lessons

Build safety before confidence.

Day 1

Learn wallet types.

Understand exchange accounts, mobile wallets, hardware wallets, watch-only wallets, and custody trade-offs.

Day 2

Understand seed phrases.

Learn what a recovery phrase controls, how people lose it, and why screenshots are dangerous.

Day 3

Secure exchange accounts.

Review passwords, two-factor authentication, withdrawal allowlists, phishing, and support impersonation.

Day 4

Practice a small test.

Use a tiny amount to learn address checking, fees, confirmations, and waiting before sending more.

Day 5

Run the scam checklist.

Check fake wallets, fake support, fake airdrops, fake recovery services, and urgency pressure.

Day 6

Create a custody plan.

Decide where small, medium, and long-term holdings belong and what happens if a device is lost.

Day 7

Document without exposing secrets.

Keep exchange exports and purchase records. Never store seed phrases in ordinary notes or cloud screenshots.

Safety note

This is wallet-security education only, not financial, investment, legal, tax, or custody advice. Never share seed phrases or private keys.
