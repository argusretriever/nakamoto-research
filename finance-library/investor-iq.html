Assessment knowledge

Investor IQ should diagnose behavior

A useful assessment identifies habits, blind spots, and next lessons instead of flattering the user.

Measure process

Good investor skill includes planning, risk control, source use, review cadence, and behavior.

Example: high knowledge with poor behavior still creates risk.

  • Mistake: scoring confidence instead of process.
Route by weakness

Each answer should point to a relevant lesson or tool.

Example: low source discipline routes to source policy and research library.

  • Mistake: giving everyone the same result.
Retake periodically

Investor skill changes with experience and market cycles.

Example: retake after a quarter of using the dashboard.

  • Mistake: treating one score as identity.
