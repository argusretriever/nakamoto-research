Treasury publishes July UK reserve holdings

Published 16 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-08-05

The Treasury release describes movements in official reserves during July. The holdings include foreign currency assets, gold and assets held through the IMF.

How to read the release

Compare the opening and closing values using the same currency. Check the release’s explanation of valuation changes before treating the difference as a purchase or sale.
