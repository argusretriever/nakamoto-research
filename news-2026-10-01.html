How to read the ONS October real-time indicators release

Published 16 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-10-01

The ONS released an update on 1 October covering rapid indicators drawn from surveys and other data sources. To compare observations across releases, record the series, frequency, coverage and reference period. A rapidly published indicator can add context ahead of a slower official series, but different measures can diverge.
