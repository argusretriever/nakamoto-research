Comparison

ETF vs mutual fund: understand the wrapper.

Both can hold diversified assets. The differences are usually trading, pricing, minimums, fees, tax treatment, transparency, and platform availability.

ETF

Trades like a stock.

Intraday trading, bid-ask spread, market orders, limit orders, usually transparent holdings, and exchange liquidity.

Mutual Fund

Prices after market close.

Often bought through fund platforms with end-of-day pricing, possible minimums, and different fee structures.

Decision

Choose by purpose.

Check cost, tax wrapper, platform, liquidity, holding period, and whether the fund fits your portfolio policy.
