Clinic framework

Portfolio clinic knowledge

A portfolio review should diagnose role, risk, overlap, cost, taxes, and behavior before suggesting changes.

Start with objectives

Know what the portfolio is supposed to fund and when.

Example: retirement income, wealth growth, down payment, or education savings.

  • Mistake: reviewing holdings without goals.
Find hidden overlap

Look through funds to understand actual exposure.

Example: multiple large-cap ETFs can concentrate the same companies.

  • Mistake: assuming fund count equals diversification.
Document the review

Every clinic should end with keep, simplify, rebalance, research, or no action.

Example: no action is valid when policy rules are still met.

  • Mistake: changing something just to feel productive.
