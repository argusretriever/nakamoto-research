Important risk note

Capital is at risk with investing. Funded-trading and evaluation programs can include fees, drawdown rules, payout conditions, prohibited strategies, account restrictions, and no guarantee of profit. Business cards, payment processors, and business finance tools can include eligibility rules, fees, repayment obligations, settlement timing, chargebacks, credit checks, and changing terms. Referral rewards, protections, account features, fees, tax treatment, and product availability can change. Always read official terms before opening an account. This page is educational and commercial disclosure, not financial advice.

Reader Checks

Before clicking a referral link, check these.

Fit

Does the platform match the reader's goal: spending, saving, ETF investing, stock investing, or simple account management?

Fees

Check account fees, trading fees, FX fees, card fees, fund costs, spreads, and withdrawal or transfer costs.

Risk

Investments can lose value. Do not sign up because of a reward without understanding the product and downside.

Records

Keep account statements, trade history, dividend records, tax documents, referral rewards, and fee records.

Trading

For funded-trading links, check evaluation fees, drawdown limits, payout rules, platform restrictions, and whether the rules fit your trading plan.

Business

For business payment and credit-card links, check fees, repayment terms, chargebacks, settlement times, rewards conditions, and accounting records.
