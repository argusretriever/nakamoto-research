Build a borrowing-versus-debt evidence card

A borrowing headline becomes more useful when the reader separates a monthly flow from a year-to-date flow and a debt stock.

Published 27 October 2026 as a retrospective study for the 25 October archive slot. Original source: ONS public sector finances, UK: September 2026, published 21 October 2026. This article was not published on 25 October.

Create three rows

From the ONS release, write one row for September borrowing (£24.5 billion), one for borrowing in the financial year to September, and one for debt at the end of September (provisionally above £3 trillion). Add a column for each row’s period and whether it is a stock or flow.

Add the right comparisons

The monthly figure is £1.8 billion higher than September 2025. The financial-year figure is lower than the equivalent period a year earlier but above the OBR forecast. Do not describe those distinct comparisons as one directional move.

Write a cautious takeaway

The bulletin describes public finance estimates, not a personal tax bill or an investment signal. Check revisions and the debt-to-GDP ratio before repeating a headline.
