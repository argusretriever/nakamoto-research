Reader safety

RWA pages can be high risk because legal rights may differ from marketing language. Always verify the legal entity, contract terms, redemption path, and regulator status before trusting a tokenized claim.

Issuer Scorecard

What the database should record for every RWA issuer.

Entity

Who is the legal issuer?

Record company name, jurisdiction, regulator status, parent company, and public contact route.

Asset

What does the token represent?

Stock, Treasury bill, fund share, invoice, real estate claim, commodity, or internal points system.

Rights

What rights does the holder receive?

Legal title, contractual claim, synthetic exposure, dividend treatment, voting, and transfer limits.

Custody

Where is the underlying asset held?

Check custodian identity, account segregation, audit language, and bankruptcy treatment.

Redemption

Can holders exit at the source?

Record who can redeem, minimums, fees, delays, freeze rights, and secondary-market dependency.

Proof

Which documents support the claim?

Prospectus, terms, reserves, auditor report, regulator filing, smart contract, and issuer disclosures.
