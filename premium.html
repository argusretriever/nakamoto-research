Research Trust

How premium research should be made.

Source-led

Use official sources, filings, primary documents, credible datasets, and clear source trails wherever possible.

Transparent

Separate facts, interpretation, uncertainty, and opinion. Mark updates and corrections clearly.

Education-first

Explain the decision framework instead of telling readers what to buy, sell, or trade.

Risk-aware

Show downside, limitations, conflicts, affiliate context, custody risk, and tax/accounting boundaries.
