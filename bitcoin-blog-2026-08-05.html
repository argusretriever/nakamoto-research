Stress-test a mining spreadsheet

Vary assumptions independently so the weak point is visible.

Archive slot: 5 August 2026
Published 13 September 2026. This educational retrospective fills an archive gap. It is not a report of market conditions on the archive date.

Source foundation

Mining uses proof of work; pooling can reduce payout variability. Bitcoin developer guide: mining.

A worked example

A hypothetical model has daily receipts of 12 units and expenses of 9. A 25 percent fall in receipts removes the entire three-unit margin. A separate increase in expenses to 11 leaves only one unit. Combining those scenarios creates a loss of two units.

Original hypothetical example. Amounts, people, events, and scenarios above are invented for learning; they are not historical observations or forecasts.

What to take from it

Use sensitivity analysis to identify assumptions, not to claim a forecast.

Try it on paper

Make a two-by-two table of high and low receipts and expenses.

Keep the assumptions beside your answer. Check the result before applying the idea to a different situation.
