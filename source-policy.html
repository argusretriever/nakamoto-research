Source Confidence Labels

How to read our source trail.

Official filing

Highest confidence for legal or issuer claims.

Examples include SEC filings, regulator notices, court documents, ETF filings, and company regulatory disclosures.

Regulator source

Primary policy material.

Examples include SEC, CFTC, FCA, Treasury, central bank, and legislative pages.

Company release

Useful but self-interested.

Company announcements should be checked against filings, data, and third-party context when material.

Market data

Point-in-time numbers.

Prices, flows, yields, and dashboards can change quickly and should be timestamped or treated as snapshots.

Primary document

Original text beats summary.

Whitepapers, GitHub repositories, technical docs, and protocol posts are preferred for technical claims.

Media report

Attributed reporting with context.

Media reports can be useful, but important claims should be traced to primary documents where possible.

Reader Route

Verify before acting.

For wallets, ETFs, RWA, stablecoins, and policy, readers should check the original source before making financial, legal, or security decisions.
