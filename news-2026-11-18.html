UK inflation rose in October as producer costs increased

Published 18 November 2026. Sources checked at 23:47 London.

The October ONS releases show higher annual consumer and producer-price inflation. Compare the published rates and check revisions to September.

Consumer prices

Annual CPI inflation was 3.6%, up from 3.5% in September. CPIH, which includes owner occupiers’ housing costs, was 3.7%. Both indices increased 0.5% over the month.

Producer prices and revisions

Manufacturers’ input prices rose 10.8% over the year, while factory-gate prices rose 5.1%. The September output rate was revised to 4.7% from the 4.6% reported in its first release. September and October PPI estimates remain provisional.
