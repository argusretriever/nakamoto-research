How to read the November Bank of England decision

Published 5 November 2026

The Bank of England scheduled its November decision and Monetary Policy Report for noon London time on 5 November. Both official pages still showed release notices at the 02:00 London source check.

What to extract at release

Read the policy summary for the rate decision and the minutes for the vote and reasoning. Use the Monetary Policy Report to record the assumptions behind its projections. Keep the decision date separate from the period covered by each forecast.
