Prepare an event-review card

On paper, write the event, the evidence you will read, and a condition that would make you pause or revisit an interpretation. Keep a separate column for the observed market response. A scheduled announcement is not a direction signal; the exercise is to make the review process explicit.

FAQ

Trading Academy questions.

Signals

Does Trading Academy provide trade signals?

No. It teaches market structure, risk, vocabulary, and process. It does not tell readers what to buy, sell, or trade.

Beginner

Where should a beginner start?

Start with Trading Start Here, then read the glossary, market structure lesson, and risk lab before using drills or scenarios.

Risk

Why risk first?

Risk controls, invalidation, sizing, and review discipline reduce impulsive decisions and make learning more durable.

Scope

Is this only for Bitcoin?

No. The lessons teach general market education, then connect useful context back to Bitcoin, crypto, macro, and risk appetite.
