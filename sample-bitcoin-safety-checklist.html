Safety note

This sample is educational only. Do not share seed phrases, private keys, wallet files, recovery words, or exchange login details with anyone.

Checklist

Before moving meaningful funds.

Wallet

Wallet readiness.

Understand hot wallet vs hardware wallet, device trust, update source, official app links, and recovery model.

Seed

Seed phrase backup.

Write recovery words offline, verify order, store separately, avoid screenshots, cloud storage, printers, and chat apps.

Exchange

Withdrawal check.

Confirm network, address, fee, minimum withdrawal, whitelist settings, two-factor authentication, and support route.

Test

Test transaction.

Send a small amount first, wait for confirmation, verify receiving address, and document the process.

Scams

Scam filter.

Ignore recovery helpers, fake support, airdrops, wallet verification links, private-message offers, and urgency traps.

Recovery

Recovery plan.

Plan what happens if a device is lost, damaged, stolen, or if trusted family needs access later.
