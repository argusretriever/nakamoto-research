USA Guide

Use records before opinions.

Organize IRS-facing records, brokerage reports, retirement-account questions, digital asset activity, mortgage documents, and state-level differences.

Checklist

What a US reader should check.

Tax

Start with IRS digital asset and tax records.

Track income, gains, losses, forms, cost basis, account statements, and professional questions.

Investing

Understand account type first.

Separate taxable brokerage accounts, retirement accounts, fund fees, risk, and withdrawal rules before product selection.

Bitcoin

Keep digital asset evidence.

Save exchange exports, wallet transaction records, fees, transfers, acquisition notes, and sale records.

Property

Model mortgage pressure with taxes and insurance.

Housing decisions need property taxes, insurance, maintenance, HOA costs, rates, and income resilience.

Business

Separate personal and business records.

Keep invoices, receipts, bank records, contracts, software exports, payroll notes, and entity documents.

Sources

Use official sources first.

Prioritize IRS, SEC, FINRA, CFPB, Federal Reserve, state regulators, and provider documents.

USA education note

This is education only and not US tax, legal, mortgage, investment, crypto, or accounting advice. Check current official sources and qualified professionals.
