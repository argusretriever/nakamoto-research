ETF Basics

What is a spot Bitcoin ETF?

In the U.S., the SEC approved listing and trading of spot bitcoin exchange-traded product shares in January 2024. These products trade like securities, but the underlying exposure is tied to bitcoin.

Access

Brokerage account exposure.

Investors can buy shares through familiar brokerage platforms instead of opening an exchange account or managing private keys.

Custody

You own shares, not keys.

The fund structure handles custody. That lowers wallet risk for some users but removes direct control over bitcoin.

Tracking

Watch fees and spreads.

Expense ratios, premiums or discounts, market hours, liquidity, and tax treatment can affect realized results.

Decision Check

ETF or self-custody?

ETF may fit

Brokerage accounts, retirement accounts, simpler tax records, and users who do not want key-management responsibility.

Self-custody may fit

Direct ownership, 24/7 transfer ability, censorship resistance, personal control, and users willing to manage backups carefully.

Both have risk

ETFs have issuer and custody dependencies. Self-custody has irreversible mistake, theft, and inheritance risk.
