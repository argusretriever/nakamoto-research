Case study

Trend day: when continuation matters more than prediction.

A trend day trains patience. The lesson is not to guess the top or bottom, but to understand structure, pullbacks, and risk-defined continuation.

Context

Opening drive creates directional control. Pullbacks are shallow. Prior resistance becomes support. Countertrend attempts fail quickly.

Student task

Mark every continuation pullback, invalidation level, and area where chasing would create poor reward/risk.

Professional lesson

Trend days punish mean-reversion bias. The process is to define participation points, not force reversal narratives.
