Worksheet Fields

Fill this before building.

Customer

Who exactly has this problem?

Audience, role, situation, urgency, and where they already spend attention.

Pain

What does the problem cost?

Money, time, risk, stress, lost opportunity, or repeated frustration.

Current Workaround

What do they do now?

Manual process, competitor, spreadsheet, avoidance, forum advice, or existing service.

Offer

What is the smallest paid solution?

Audit, consultation, template, service, tool, guide, cohort, or productized offer.

Price

What price tests seriousness?

Pick a price that creates real commitment, not only interest.

Proof

What proof will count?

Deposit, order, booked call, reply, usage, referral, or repeat behaviour.
