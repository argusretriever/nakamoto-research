## Quality Control, Risk Prevention, and Editorial Safety Tasks

1. Do not let AI output make the site generic; every article needs a specific angle, source trail, and reader value.

6. Require evidence for every big claim, especially market, legal, security, exchange, custody, policy, and investment-adjacent claims.
7. Separate fact from interpretation; label official facts, reported claims, market reactions, and editorial analysis clearly.
8. Avoid false certainty; use careful wording when outcomes, legal interpretations, prices, or risks are uncertain.
9. Avoid accidental financial advice; provide education, context, and risk framing without telling readers what to buy, sell, or hold.
