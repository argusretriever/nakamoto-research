Trading journal

Make every trade produce information.

A journal is not a diary. It is a feedback system for setup quality, rule adherence, risk behavior, emotions, and review.

Before Trade

Plan

  • Market and timeframe
  • Setup name
  • Market condition
  • Entry logic
  • Invalidation level
  • Risk percent
During Trade

Manage

  • Did I follow entry rules?
  • Did I move the stop?
  • Did I scale by plan?
  • Did news or volatility change conditions?
After Trade

Review

  • Result in R
  • Screenshot before and after
  • Emotion tag
  • Mistake tag
  • One lesson
  • One rule adjustment
Weekly Review

Score

  • Best trade by process
  • Worst trade by process
  • Most common mistake
  • Average R
  • Rule adherence score
  • Next week focus
