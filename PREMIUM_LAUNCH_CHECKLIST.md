# Nakamoto Premium Launch Checklist

Internal only. Do not publish as a website page.

## Before Checkout

1. Finalize tier names and prices.
2. Finalize refund policy.
3. Finalize cancellation wording.
4. Finalize support email and response expectations.
5. Prepare first-month content.
6. Choose payment provider.
7. Test recurring billing.
8. Test cancellation flow.
9. Test receipts and failed-payment flow.
10. Test waitlist-to-member email flow.

## First-Month Content Required

1. Weekly Pro Brief sample.
2. Monthly Wealth Plan.
3. Trading Journal pack.
4. Bitcoin Safety Checklist.
5. Investing checklist library.
6. Premium source archive.
7. Member welcome email.
8. Education-only disclaimer.

## Trust Requirements

1. No signals.
2. No guaranteed returns.
3. No personal advice.
4. No fake urgency.
5. Clear refund terms.
6. Clear cancellation path.
7. Clear support path.
8. Clear privacy policy.
