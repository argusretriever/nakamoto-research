Exchange risk

Crypto exchanges can involve custody risk, withdrawal risk, compliance risk, account restrictions, security incidents, high volatility, fees, and product availability limits. Futures and leverage can cause rapid losses. Check official terms, fee pages, jurisdiction rules, and risk warnings before acting.

Pre-Use Checklist

Check these before opening any exchange product.

Country

Confirm the product is available and lawful where you live.

Identity

Understand KYC requirements, restricted regions, account reviews, and withdrawal limits.

Custody

Exchange balances are custodial. Plan withdrawals and self-custody before holding meaningful funds.

Security

Use strong passwords, app-based 2FA, withdrawal allowlists, anti-phishing codes, and device hygiene.

Fees

Check spot fees, futures fees, funding rates, spreads, card fees, deposit fees, and withdrawal fees.

Tax Records

Keep trade history, deposits, withdrawals, fees, funding, card transactions, and statements for tax records.

Futures

Leverage can liquidate positions quickly. Do not use futures without margin, funding, stop, and liquidation knowledge.

Exit Plan

Know how to withdraw funds, close positions, export records, and contact support before depositing.
