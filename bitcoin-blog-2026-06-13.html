A wallet balance has separate pieces

Draw the pieces behind a displayed balance before deciding what can be spent.

Archive slot: 13 June 2026
Published 13 September 2026. This educational retrospective fills an archive gap. It is not a report of market conditions on the archive date.

Source foundation

Ordinary Bitcoin transactions spend earlier outputs and create new outputs. Bitcoin developer guide: transactions.

A worked example

Imagine two unspent outputs of 40,000 and 70,000 satoshis. A payment needs 80,000 satoshis plus a hypothetical 1,000-satoshi fee. Using both inputs leaves 29,000 satoshis as change. Check the arithmetic: 110,000 equals 80,000 plus 29,000 plus 1,000.

Original hypothetical example. Amounts, people, events, and scenarios above are invented for learning; they are not historical observations or forecasts.

What to take from it

A balance alone does not describe the transaction that a wallet will construct.

Try it on paper

Draw a second payment that spends only the 70,000-satoshi output.

Keep the assumptions beside your answer. Check the result before applying the idea to a different situation.
