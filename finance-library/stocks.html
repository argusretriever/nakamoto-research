Stock knowledge

Stock research should become a written thesis

A stock page should help readers understand the business, cash flows, valuation, risks, and what would prove the thesis wrong.

Study business quality

Look for durable demand, pricing power, reinvestment opportunities, balance sheet strength, and management capital allocation.

Example: high margins mean little if revenue is declining or debt is fragile.

  • Mistake: buying only because a stock is down.
Use valuation ranges

Valuation is a range of outcomes, not one perfect number.

Example: compare base, bear, and bull cases using margins, growth, and multiples.

  • Mistake: relying on a single P/E ratio.
Write update triggers

Decide what evidence would make you add, hold, trim, or exit before price volatility arrives.

Example: margin deterioration, debt increase, thesis failure, or better opportunity.

  • Mistake: changing the thesis to justify the price.
