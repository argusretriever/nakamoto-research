Curriculum

A complete trading education path from foundations to professional process.

The curriculum is organized by learning level, not random topics. Each level builds vocabulary, context, risk awareness, practice routines, and review discipline.

Education only

NAKAMOTO Trading Academy teaches market structure, risk, vocabulary, and process. It does not provide trade signals, investment advice, profit guarantees, or personal financial recommendations.

10 Levels

Build the foundation before seeking advanced patterns.

Level 0

Foundations

Financial markets, asset classes, market participants, order types, liquidity, volatility, and basic mechanics.

Level 1

Technical Analysis

Market structure, trends, support and resistance, price action, trendlines, breaks, and retests.

Level 2

Auction Theory

Price discovery, balance, imbalance, acceptance, rejection, volume profile, value areas, and migration.

Level 3

Order Flow

Volume, delta, cumulative delta, footprint concepts, order book behavior, absorption, and exhaustion.

Level 4

Liquidity Concepts

Liquidity pools, sweeps, structural breaks, fair value gaps, premium and discount, and session behavior.

Level 5

Advanced Technical Analysis

Multi-timeframe analysis, correlations, Fibonacci context, structured confluence, and invalidation planning.

Level 6

Quantitative Thinking

Probability, statistics, expectancy, sample size, backtesting limits, and strategy review.

Level 7

Risk Management

Position sizing, drawdown, risk limits, portfolio pressure, and capital preservation.

Level 8

Psychology

Discipline, emotional control, revenge trading, journaling, and behavior feedback loops.

Level 9

Professional Process

Research frameworks, performance reviews, decision governance, checklists, and continuous improvement.
