FAQ

ETF research FAQ.

Short answers about expense ratios, liquidity, issuer risk, holdings, overlap, tracking, taxes, and portfolio role.

Q

What should I check before buying an ETF?

Fees, liquidity, issuer, holdings, overlap, tracking, tax notes, and whether the fund has a clear portfolio role.

Q

Why do ETF fees matter?

Fees, spreads, platform charges, and tax drag compound over time and reduce net returns.

Q

Is an ETF ticker enough information?

No. The ticker is only a label. Check the fact sheet, holdings, methodology, risks, costs, and issuer documents.
