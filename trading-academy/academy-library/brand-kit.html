Export structures

Ready-to-use asset formats.

These structures define what should be exported from the website into courses, PDFs, decks, and social assets.

Lesson PDF Cover

Header: NAKAMOTO Academy mark, lesson title, level, module, reading time, risk disclaimer.

Body: objective, prerequisite, concept diagram, glossary terms, practice task, quiz, next lesson.

Worksheet Template

Header: student name, date, module, setup, risk limit, review status.

Body: chart notes, invalidation, mistakes avoided, checklist score, next improvement.

Research Report Template

Header: title, method, sample, source quality, update date, educational disclaimer.

Body: executive summary, findings, charts, caveats, citations, methodology notes.
