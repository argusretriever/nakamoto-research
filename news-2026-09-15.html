September payroll estimate showed fewer employees

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-15. Original source published on this earlier date.

The ONS September release estimated UK payrolled employees at 30.2 million in August 2026, down 145,000 from a year earlier.

What the release reported

The early monthly estimate fell by 26,000 from July. Administrative payroll data cover employees and can change as employers supply more information. This was an early August estimate available in the September release.

Match the reference periods

The same bulletin reported payroll changes for May to July, the period comparable with its Labour Force Survey estimates. Put the period beside each figure before comparing monthly administrative records with three-month survey results.
