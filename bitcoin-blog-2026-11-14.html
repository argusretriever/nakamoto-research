Published 14 November 2026

Use the ONS short-term lets bulletin to compare three measures of accommodation use.

Work through the comparison

Start with three guests staying two nights on one booking. Record six guest nights, two booked nights and one stay. In the ONS table, divide guest nights by nights to estimate the average party size per occupied night. Record the period and platform coverage beside your result.
