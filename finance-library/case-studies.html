Case study knowledge

Case studies should teach process

A useful case study explains situation, constraints, decision, tradeoffs, outcome, and what a reader can learn.

Show context

Case studies need goals, time horizon, account type, and constraints.

Example: two portfolios with same holdings can have different purposes.

  • Mistake: copying outcomes without context.
Explain tradeoffs

Every decision gives something up.

Example: more stability may mean lower expected return.

  • Mistake: presenting only benefits.
Make lessons transferable

The point is a reusable rule, not imitation.

Example: write rebalancing bands.

  • Mistake: treating a case as advice.
