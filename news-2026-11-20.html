ONS recorded more business births and fewer business deaths in 2025

Published 20 November 2026

The annual business-demography release covers business births, deaths and survival during 2025. Its source was published on 19 November 2026.

The annual counts

UK business births rose from 317,000 in 2024 to 326,000 in 2025. Business deaths fell from 283,000 to 271,000. The birth rate was 11.3% and the death rate was 9.4%.

Activity varies by industry and place

Transport and storage had the highest birth and death rates. London had the highest birth rate. These are measures of business population turnover. Compare the same industry and geographical coverage before drawing a conclusion about a particular business.

Read the period before the headline

The publication date is in November 2026, while the reference year is 2025. Use the detailed definitions and dataset alongside the headline counts when comparing this release with a more recent business survey.
