Coverage Model

What this desk covers.

Stablecoin stories should identify the issuer, reserve proof, redemption rights, chain risk, regulation, wallet safety, and user impact.

Reserves

Cash, Treasuries, attestations, audits, reserve composition, and transparency.

Never treat a dollar peg as proof without reserve and redemption context.

Redemption

Who can redeem, what breaks, which entity is liable, and how fast dollars return.

Translate legal structure into practical user risk.

Payments

Settlement, merchant use, cross-border flows, payment APIs, and wallet UX.

Track real usage separately from partnership announcements.

Regulation

Stablecoin law, licensing, bank rules, issuer supervision, and enforcement.

Route rules into the policy desk and explain practical consequences.

Depeg Risk

Liquidity stress, chain congestion, exchange outages, blacklists, and custody issues.

Build prevention lessons from every market stress event.

Issuer Risk

Tether, Circle, Paxos, First Digital, PayPal, bank issuers, and tokenized cash products.

Compare issuers by source evidence, not brand familiarity.
