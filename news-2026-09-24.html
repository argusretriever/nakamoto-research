ONS business survey highlights turnover pressures

Published 16 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-24

In its 24 September bulletin, the ONS found that 29% of trading businesses reported economic uncertainty as a turnover challenge. Among businesses with at least ten employees, labour cost was the most reported challenge at 37%.

How to read the release

These percentages refer to different groups. Record the survey population before comparing the figures, and keep reported challenges separate from measured turnover.
