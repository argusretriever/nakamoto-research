Pink Book 2026 puts the UK current-account deficit at 3.0% of GDP in 2025

Retrospective archive slot: 2026-11-01. Written 3 November 2026. Published 3 November 2026.

Original source: Office for National Statistics. 30 October 2026. Verified 3 November 2026.

What the source establishes

The Pink Book reports a UK current-account deficit of £91.6 billion in 2025, equivalent to 3.0% of GDP. The revised 2024 comparison is £84.3 billion, or 2.9%. It reports a net international investment liability position of £60.7 billion at the end of 2025. All periods were open to revision.

What it does not establish

The current account measures transactions over a period; the investment position is a balance at a date. Neither figure is a forecast for sterling, Bitcoin or an individual investment.

Read the evidence carefully

Make two evidence rows: one for a flow during a year and one for a stock at year-end. Write down the unit, sign convention, reference period and revision status before explaining either number.
