Chart drill library

Practice reading charts before risking capital.

Each drill gives a task, a decision question, a common trap, and a completion standard. Use these with screenshots, replay, or historical charts.

Structure

Mark HH, HL, LH, LL

Label the last ten meaningful swings. Ignore tiny noise. Completion: identify trend, range, or transition with one invalidation level.

Structure

Find BOS and CHoCH

Mark the level that actually changed structure. Trap: calling every wick a break. Completion: require close, displacement, or follow-through rule.

Support / Resistance

Draw zones, not magic lines

Mark reaction areas with thickness. Completion: explain whether the zone is fresh, tested, broken, or reclaimed.

Liquidity

Locate obvious stops

Mark equal highs, equal lows, swing extremes, and breakout traps. Completion: explain who may be forced to act if price sweeps the area.

Auction

Profile acceptance vs rejection

Mark value area, POC, failed acceptance, and rejection tails. Completion: state whether price is balancing, migrating, or breaking value.

Order Flow

Delta divergence drill

Compare price movement with delta behavior. Completion: identify whether aggressive participation confirms or conflicts with price.

Risk

Entry-stop-target map

Before judging a setup, mark entry logic, invalidation, target, and risk/reward. Completion: reject trades without clear invalidation.

Review

One chart, three explanations

Write bullish, bearish, and neutral interpretations. Completion: choose the one with the strongest evidence and define what would change your mind.
