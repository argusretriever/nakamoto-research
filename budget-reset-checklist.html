Money Checklist

Reset the budget before adding more financial complexity.

A budget reset is not punishment. It is a visibility exercise: what comes in, what goes out, what leaks, and what needs protection first.

Open checklistCalculatorsMoney templates

Checklist

Start with the facts.

Income

List reliable income.

Separate salary, irregular income, side income, benefits, refunds, and one-off payments.

Bills

List fixed essentials.

Housing, utilities, transport, food, insurance, minimum debt payments, and taxes.

Leaks

Find invisible spending.

Subscriptions, takeaways, fees, unused services, impulse purchases, and duplicate tools.

Safety

Set a small buffer first.

Emergency cash reduces panic and stops small shocks from becoming debt.

Monthly Review

Use four questions every month.

1

What surprised me?

Unexpected costs reveal missing categories.

2

What can be reduced?

Cut waste before cutting joy.

3

What needs a sinking fund?

Annual costs are not emergencies if planned.

Education note

This checklist is educational and not personal financial, tax, or debt advice.
