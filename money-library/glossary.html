Glossary

Plain-English money terms.

A durable literacy layer for beginners. Each term links naturally into NAKAMOTO Money Library guides, calculators, and tools.

Budget

Budget

A plan for how income will be used across bills, spending, saving, debt, and goals.

Savings

Emergency fund

Cash set aside for essential surprises, income gaps, urgent repairs, or medical costs.

Debt

APR

Annual percentage rate. A yearly cost of borrowing that helps compare debts.

Debt

Debt avalanche

A payoff method that targets the highest-interest debt first.

Debt

Debt snowball

A payoff method that targets the smallest balance first for faster visible wins.

Wealth

Net worth

Assets minus liabilities. Useful as a long-term trend, not a self-worth score.

Goals

Sinking fund

Savings set aside gradually for a known future expense.

Investing

Diversification

Spreading investments across different holdings so one result does not decide everything.

Retirement

Employer match

Money an employer may contribute to a retirement account when the worker contributes.
