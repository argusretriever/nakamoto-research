Date: 2026-06-27

Scope: visual design, graphic design, motion, illustration, iconography, UI, UX, website design, product design, brand identity, editorial design, marketing design, infographics, presentation direction, accessibility, performance, design systems, UX research, conversion, brand strategy, product strategy, scalability, AI-assisted workflows, quality review, and continuous refinement.

Benchmark principles used: Apple, Stripe, Linear, Figma, Notion, Awwwards, Red Dot, D&AD, Webby, iF, IDEA, Cannes Lions, Pentagram, and IDEO as quality references only. No layouts, logos, systems, or proprietary styles are copied.

## Executive Summary

Nakamoto Research is strongest when it behaves like a premium intelligence desk: calm, monochrome, source-first, structured, and useful. The main creative weakness was not lack of content, but inconsistent visual layers from many experiments: decorative backgrounds, mixed card styles, oversized typography, inconsistent motion, and uneven discovery presentation.

Safe improvements implemented:

1. Added a final monochrome creative QA layer in `styles.css`.
2. Standardized final card, form, button, navigation, search, focus, and hover behavior.
3. Preserved the user preference for pure black, gray, white, silver, and restrained premium detail.
4. Added motion restraint and reduced-motion safeguards.
5. Improved responsive headline scale and card rhythm.
6. Preserved the no-round-background navigation requirement.
7. Preserved the removed decorative Bitcoin-logo backing.
8. Improved search result polish and sitemap route distinction.

## What Matters Most

1. The website should feel like a premium research publication, not a template.
2. The brand should stay monochrome, calm, exact, and source-led.
3. Typography must be bold but never unreadable.
4. Cards must scan fast and never overlap.
5. Motion must support clarity, not decorate noise.
6. Search and discovery are part of design, not only SEO.
7. Accessibility is brand quality.
8. Every new page must pass visual, content, link, metadata, sitemap, and search checks.
