FAQ

Business validation FAQ.

Short answers about customer interviews, MVPs, proof of demand, deposits, pricing, and when to build.

Q

What counts as business validation?

Stronger validation comes from behaviour: deposits, paid pilots, booked calls, usage, referrals, replies, or repeated demand.

Q

Are compliments validation?

Compliments are weak signals. A real commitment is more useful than a friendly opinion.

Q

When should I build the product?

Build after you understand the customer, problem, offer, price, and proof of demand well enough to reduce avoidable waste.
