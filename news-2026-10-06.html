ONS publishes reference tables for its labour productivity analysis

Published 16 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-10-06

The ONS released reference tables on 6 October covering 1997 to 2024. They supply the absolute values behind the component approach to labour productivity and include chained volume and current-price measures.

How to read the release

Use the volume series to compare changes after removing price effects. Record whether the denominator is hours or workers before comparing results.
