UK business survey tracks concern about climate impacts

Published 15 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 8 October 2026

In its 8 October bulletin the ONS reported that 41% of businesses expressed some degree of concern about the impact of climate change, nine percentage points above June 2026.

How to read the release

The number describes survey responses. When comparing waves, record the response population and question wording. The results do not measure realised financial losses.
