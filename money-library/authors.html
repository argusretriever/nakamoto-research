Editorial rules

What every serious finance page should include

  • Clear educational scope and no personalized advice claims.
  • Last reviewed date and source list when rules or account details matter.
  • Plain-language explanation of assumptions in calculators.
  • Next steps that help users act without pressure.
  • Country-specific caveats where local rules may vary.
