Durability rules

What makes a page useful in 10 years?

  • It answers a stable human money question.
  • It explains concepts before tactics.
  • It links to official sources for rules and rights.
  • It gives examples, FAQs, tools, and next steps.
  • It can be updated without rewriting the whole page.
