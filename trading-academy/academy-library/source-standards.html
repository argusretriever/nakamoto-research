Source standards

Evidence quality rules for NAKAMOTO Academy.

Sources are ranked by reliability, relevance, transparency, and freshness. Unsupported claims are revised, cited, caveated, or removed.

Official firstAcademic when usefulCaveats requiredNo hype
AStrong Sources

Regulators, exchanges, official documentation, academic papers, textbooks, audited reports, and primary data.

BUseful Sources

Professional education pages, industry explainers, expert interviews, and research reports with transparent methodology.

CWeak Sources

Anonymous claims, social posts, screenshots without context, affiliate material, and performance claims without evidence.

DAction Rule

When evidence is weak, the page must say so or avoid making the claim.
