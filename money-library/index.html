Put a delay into a practice budget

A fictional household starts with 500 units, pays 400 on day five, and expects 900 on day ten. If income arrives on day twenty and another 150-unit bill falls on day twelve, the interim shortfall is 50 units. Draw the running balance under both schedules. This example explains timing; it is not a recommended emergency-fund target.
