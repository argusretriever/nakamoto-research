ONS business survey reported weaker turnover expectations for December

Published 20 November 2026

The 19 November business survey separates reports about October turnover from expectations for December. This review was prepared on 20 November.

Retrospective archive slot 19 November 2026.

Reported turnover and expectations

In October, 14% of trading businesses reported turnover growth over the previous month. A quarter expected turnover to decrease in December, up eight percentage points from expectations for November.

Check the population being measured

The survey also gives estimates for businesses with ten or more employees. Keep those separate from the all-trading-business results. Expectations describe respondents’ outlook at the survey date.

Use uncertainty and seasonal context

BICS is a voluntary fortnightly survey and its estimates are official statistics in development. The ONS notes that the December expectations movement is in line with patterns at this time in earlier years. Check the linked confidence intervals before treating a small difference as decisive.
