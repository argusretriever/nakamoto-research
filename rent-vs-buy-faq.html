Rent vs buy FAQ.

Short answers about total cost, deposit pressure, maintenance, mobility, rates, and opportunity cost.

Q

Is buying always better than renting?

No. The answer depends on total cost, time horizon, deposit, maintenance, taxes, mobility, rates, and opportunity cost.

Q

What costs do buyers often forget?

Taxes, legal fees, insurance, maintenance, service charges, repairs, moving costs, and cash left after deposit.

Q

Why does mobility matter?

Buying can become expensive if you need to move soon because transaction costs and market risk may outweigh the benefits.
