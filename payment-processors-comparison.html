Decision Table

Compare the operating cost, not only the headline fee.

In-person sales

Card reader and terminal fit.

Check reader cost, battery life, connection, receipt flow, tipping, refunds, offline behavior, and staff training.

Online sales

Checkout and link payments.

Check checkout pages, payment links, invoices, failed-payment handling, tax receipts, and customer support workflow.

Cash flow

Settlement timing.

Slow payout can create pressure even when sales are strong. Compare payout timing, holds, reserves, and bank transfer rules.

Risk

Refunds and chargebacks.

Understand dispute evidence, chargeback fees, frozen funds, fraud checks, and how records are exported.

Product Table

Payment processor decision table.

Best for

Mobile and small in-person sales.

Market stalls, mobile services, pop-ups, events, and small teams that need simple card acceptance.

Key risks

Chargebacks and settlement holds.

Funds can be delayed or disputed. Refunds and chargebacks need evidence and clean records.

Fees to verify

Hardware, transaction, payout.

Check card-present fees, online fees, hardware cost, refund costs, chargeback fees, and payout speed.

Records needed

Settlement and tax trail.

Export sales, fees, refunds, disputes, payout batches, invoices, and VAT or sales-tax records where relevant.

Avoid if

You need complex enterprise flows.

High-volume subscriptions, custom checkout, international complexity, or advanced integrations may need deeper tooling.
