Risk note

Stablecoins are not all the same. Reserve quality, redemption access, jurisdiction, issuer structure, chain support, and exchange liquidity can change the real risk profile.

Risk Matrix

Six checks before trusting a dollar token.

Reserve

What assets back the token?

Cash, Treasury bills, repos, funds, loans, commercial paper, or unclear assets create different risk.

Report

How recent is the attestation?

Reports should be dated, easy to find, and specific enough to compare supply with reserve assets.

Redeem

Who can redeem directly?

Retail holders may rely on exchanges if direct issuer redemption is limited.

Issuer

Which legal entity issues it?

Jurisdiction, regulation, bankruptcy treatment, and terms of service all matter.

Chain

Where does the token circulate?

Bridge, blacklist, smart-contract, and chain-congestion risk can be separate from reserve risk.

Stress

What happened during depegs?

Past stress events reveal liquidity, communication quality, and exchange dependency.
