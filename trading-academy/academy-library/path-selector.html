Path selector FAQ

Use the path as a starting point.

The selector helps reduce confusion; it does not replace deliberate practice.

What if I fit multiple paths?

Choose the path connected to your biggest current weakness. Most learners rotate between vocabulary, chart skill, risk, and behavior.

Should advanced learners skip basics?

No. Advanced mistakes often come from weak basics: unclear invalidation, poor sizing, or vague market context.

How often should I retake it?

Retake it after a completed module, a drawdown review, or when your main learning problem changes.

What comes after a recommendation?

Open the recommended page, complete one practice task, then record the result in the journal.
