August UK CPI inflation rose to 3.1%

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-16. Original source published on this earlier date.

The ONS reported UK consumer prices rose 3.1% in the year to August 2026, compared with 2.9% in July.

What the release reported

CPIH, which includes owner occupiers’ housing costs, rose 3.3% over the year. Both CPI and CPIH increased 0.5% over the month. Transport, particularly motor fuels, made the largest upward contribution to the change in their annual rates.

Label the index and comparison

The increase from 2.9% to 3.1% is 0.2 percentage points in annual CPI inflation. State whether a comparison uses CPI or CPIH and whether the period is one month or one year. A price index records accumulated changes in prices.
