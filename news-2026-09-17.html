Bank of England held Bank Rate at 3.75% in September

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-17. Original source published on this earlier date.

The Monetary Policy Committee voted 6–3 to hold Bank Rate at 3.75% at its September 2026 meeting. Three members preferred a quarter-point increase.

What the release reported

The meeting ended on 16 September and the decision was published on 17 September. The Bank discussed elevated energy prices and upside risks to inflation. Its assessment depended on the duration and effects of the energy shock.

Read the decision with its date

This article reviews the September meeting. The Bank subsequently increased Bank Rate to 4% on 5 November. Use the dated minutes to reconstruct the September decision and the later publication for a current-rate comparison.
