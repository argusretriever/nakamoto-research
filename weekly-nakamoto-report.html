Weekly rhythm

This page is designed to become the recurring report hub. Daily news creates freshness; the weekly report turns that work into memory, comparison, and a bookmark-worthy reader habit.

Report Format

Every weekly report should answer seven questions.

01

What changed in Bitcoin?

Price context, network context, ETF demand, macro pressure, and whether the move had source confirmation.

02

What did ETF flows signal?

Separate price-driven AUM changes from creations, redemptions, sponsor updates, and issuer documents.

03

What RWA claim needs proof?

Legal title, custodian, redemption path, securities status, and whether marketing language matches documents.

04

Which stablecoin risk changed?

Reserve reports, attestation timing, depeg stress, issuer terms, chain support, and redemption language.

05

What policy or security issue matters?

Regulator updates, court actions, exchange warnings, wallet drainers, phishing, fake apps, and recovery scams.

06

Which evergreen guide was improved?

Each weekly report should point to one durable page that got stronger because of the news.

07

What should readers watch next?

Give readers a calm watchlist: source, signal, risk, and next page to open.
