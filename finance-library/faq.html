How NAKAMOTO Finance Library works.

These answers help visitors understand the platform before they use research, tools, calculators, or newsletters.

Does NAKAMOTO Finance Library provide personalized investment advice?

No. NAKAMOTO Finance Library provides educational research, frameworks, tools, and market context. It does not provide personalized investment, tax, legal, or financial planning advice.

How often is market content updated?

The platform is structured for daily market notes, weekly reports, monthly recaps, and evergreen guide refreshes. Production teams should update timestamps and archive links when new pieces are published.

Are calculators and simulators exact forecasts?

No. Tools and calculators are educational estimates. They simplify assumptions and should be used to understand tradeoffs, not to predict investment outcomes.

Can I use NAKAMOTO Finance Library for stock or ETF research?

Yes. NAKAMOTO Finance Library includes stock, ETF, sector, portfolio, thesis, and research-report systems designed to support disciplined analysis and better questions.

Does NAKAMOTO Finance Library support trading education?

Yes. The Trading Academy emphasizes market structure, technical analysis, risk management, position sizing, psychology, journaling, backtesting, and execution discipline over speculation.

Do I need an account?

The current static experience stores progress locally in the browser for tools, journeys, alerts, and saved resources. Future account features should add authentication, registration, and password reset flows before launch.

How does search work?

Search uses the site content index to surface hubs, articles, tools, reports, newsletters, archives, and publishing systems. It can later be connected to a CMS or dedicated search service.

How does NAKAMOTO Finance Library handle privacy?

Current interactive state is stored locally in the visitor's browser. Newsletter and account integrations should be reviewed against the privacy policy before production launch.

How do I report a correction?

Send correction notes through the contact page. Include the page URL, the issue, and any supporting context so the editorial team can review it quickly.

Are there external APIs or live market feeds?

The current site uses static educational content and local interactive tools. Any future live-market API should include loading states, error states, data-source labels, and disclosure language.

What should readers do after finishing a guide?

Use the related tools, glossary links, research lab, model portfolios, article archive, and learning paths to turn reading into a repeatable decision process.

How can I contact NAKAMOTO Finance Library?

Use the contact page for corrections, editorial suggestions, newsletter support, platform issues, privacy questions, and partnerships.
