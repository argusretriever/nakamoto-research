The daily brief turns market noise into a calmer investor checklist.

This sample shows the format: headlines, source links, investor context, what changed, what not to overreact to, and what to read next.

Opening read

What changed overnight?

Rates, inflation expectations, earnings revisions, ETF flows, and major filings are reviewed as inputs. The brief separates verified events from interpretation so readers can decide what deserves attention.

RatesWatch duration-sensitive assets.
EarningsCompare margins with expectations.
FlowsETF demand can confirm or contradict narrative.
Three sourced signals

Headlines with context, not urgency.

  1. Inflation data: review CPI trend, core measures, and purchasing-power assumptions.
  2. Fed calendar: track statements, projections, minutes, and rate-sensitive portfolio areas.
  3. Company filings: read 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K updates before reacting to commentary.
Portfolio question

What should this make me review?

The answer is rarely "trade immediately." A better daily question is: does this news change an assumption in my written plan, watchlist, risk budget, or rebalancing rule?
