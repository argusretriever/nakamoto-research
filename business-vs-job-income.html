Earning Power

Comparison

Business income and job income solve different problems.

A job may offer stability and learning. A business may offer upside and control. Both carry risk, and many people use both during transition.

Job Income

Stability and skill compounding.

Regular pay, clearer benefits, structured learning, and lower operating complexity, but limited control and capped upside.

Business Income

Upside and ownership.

More control and scalable upside, but more uncertainty, tax complexity, customer risk, and cash-flow pressure.

Bridge

Build safely.

Use a job to fund savings, learning, and small experiments before depending on business income.

Education note

This comparison is educational and not career, legal, tax, business, or financial advice.
