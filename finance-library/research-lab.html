Lab discipline

Research lab knowledge

A research lab should teach how to form hypotheses, test evidence, document uncertainty, and update conclusions.

Hypothesis first

Start with a clear claim that can be tested.

Example: lower fees improve long-term ETF outcomes all else equal.

  • Mistake: collecting data without a question.
Use opposing evidence

Good research tries to disprove itself.

Example: compare bull and bear cases.

  • Mistake: confirmation bias.
Document versioning

Research changes as data changes.

Example: update thesis after earnings or macro releases.

  • Mistake: treating old research as permanent.
