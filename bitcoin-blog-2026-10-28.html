Separate an inflation target from a rate decision

A document can define the MPC’s job without announcing what the MPC has decided at a meeting.

Published 30 October 2026. Original source: 28 October MPC remit letter, published 28 October 2026. The 28 October date is an archive slot; this article was not published that day.

Make a two-column note

In the 28 October remit, put “2% annual CPI inflation, symmetric target” under framework. Leave the latest rate decision column for a separately dated MPC statement and minutes. Do not copy the framework into the decision column.

Read the tolerance language carefully

The letter discusses explanatory letters if inflation is more than one percentage point away from target. It says that threshold triggers accountability, not a target range or automatic rate change.

Use the method elsewhere

When a policy headline appears, record document type, issuer, publication date, and whether it is a proposal, remit, speech, forecast, or decision. That stops an ordinary framework update from becoming a false market signal.

Check the original document

Use the publisher’s source for later corrections and full definitions. Continue with the companion review, 30 October source pack, or the dated archive.
