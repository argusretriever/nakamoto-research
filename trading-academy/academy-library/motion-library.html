Rules

Motion rules for a serious trading education brand.

Purpose First

Animation must show order, consequence, attention, or feedback. Decorative motion is removed.

Calm Timing

Education pages use deliberate pacing: fast enough to feel alive, slow enough to remain legible.

Risk Respect

Risk, warnings, and disclosures are never hidden behind playful transitions or distracting movement.

Reduced Motion

Every repeated animation has a reduced-motion fallback so the site remains accessible and stable.

Progress Feedback

Scroll progress, active section options, selected filters, and calculator outputs give learners orientation.

Performance Budget

Motion uses CSS transforms, opacity, lightweight SVG, and small DOM patterns instead of heavy media.
