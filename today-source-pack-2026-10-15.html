Check the document before the interpretation

These original Federal Reserve pages were reviewed for the 15 October edition. Their release dates differ from this pack's publication date. Recheck each page for corrections or later developments.

Published survey · 9 October

2025 Survey of Consumer Finances release. The Fed reports real median family income up 7% and real mean family income down 6% between the 2022 and 2025 surveys. The debt-payment-to-income share above 40% rose from 6.5% to 8.6%. This describes survey data, not a current market quote.

Published minutes · 7 October

Minutes for the September 15–16 FOMC meeting. These minutes were published on 7 October and describe a September meeting. Do not label them as a new October policy decision.

Public-comment proposals · 24 September

Payment stablecoin proposals. The Fed requested comment on reserve, capital, risk-management, safekeeping, and application proposals. These were proposals at the cited release, not final requirements.
