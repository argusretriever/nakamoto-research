Liquidity Sweep

Buy stopsRejection

Price moves through an obvious level, triggers orders, then fails back inside the prior area. The lesson is not "every sweep reverses." The lesson is to wait for rejection and invalidation.

Volume Profile Build

POC

The widest area marks where the most volume traded. Students should compare value, acceptance, rejection, and migration instead of treating the POC as magic.

Delta Pressure

Effort vs result

Delta helps show aggressive buying and selling. The important question is whether aggressive effort actually produces price progress.
