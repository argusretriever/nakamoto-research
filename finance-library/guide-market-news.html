Most market news should change a question, not a portfolio.

Use a simple action, review, save, ignore system to separate useful market context from noise.

Guide mapFrameworkExamplesSourcesNext
Framework

Four labels for every headline.

ActionOnly when a written rule triggers.
ReviewWhen an assumption deserves checking.
SaveWhen it helps future research.
IgnoreWhen it changes nothing material.
Examples

Translate events into investor questions.

  1. CPI: review real-return assumptions, cash yield, and bond duration.
  2. FOMC: review rate sensitivity, cash, bonds, and borrowing costs.
  3. GDP: review growth assumptions and sector earnings sensitivity.
  4. Company filing: review thesis, risks, and update triggers.
