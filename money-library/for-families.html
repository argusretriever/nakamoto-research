Family meeting script

Keep it short and neutral.

Start with: "What needs to be protected before next payday?" Then review bills, food, debt minimums, and one goal. End with one decision, not ten.

Trust builder

Make roles visible.

One person may pay bills while another manages shopping or child costs. A shared map reduces resentment because hidden labor becomes visible.
