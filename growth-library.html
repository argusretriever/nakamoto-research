04 / Products

Bitcoin ETF Center.

ETF pages should teach the wrapper, not just the ticker. A Bitcoin ETF gives brokerage exposure; it is not the same as self-custody.

Spot ETFs

Price exposure through a fund.

Key questions: fees, custodian, creation/redemption, tracking, liquidity, tax reporting, and issuer risk.

Open ETF guide
Futures ETFs

Derivative exposure.

Futures products can diverge from spot returns because of roll costs, margin, fund structure, and futures curve shape.

Compare wrappers
Income ETFs

Yield with trade-offs.

Covered-call or income structures can cap upside, add complexity, and behave differently than direct spot exposure.

Read ETF news
ETF Flows

Demand signal, not destiny.

Flows can reveal institutional demand and distribution, but they do not guarantee price direction by themselves.

05 / Institutions

Bitcoin Treasury Center.

Treasury research should classify the holder before judging the holding: pure treasury company, miner, exchange, fund, operating company, or government reserve.

Pure Treasury Companies

BTC per share matters.

Track financing, dilution, custody, disclosure quality, and whether the company increases Bitcoin per share over time.

Open research
Miners

Industrial risk plus BTC exposure.

Mining treasuries depend on power cost, fleet efficiency, debt, hash price, operational uptime, and treasury policy.

Market context
Operating Companies

Bitcoin is not the whole business.

Separate operating earnings, strategic product alignment, treasury allocation, and mark-to-market accounting effects.

Business guide
Funds and Governments

Different mandates.

Funds seek investable exposure; governments may hold reserves, seized assets, strategic stockpiles, or policy positions.

Policy page

06 / Security

Bitcoin Security Alerts.

Security alerts create repeat visits because threat patterns change: fake apps, phishing, malware, compromised social accounts, fake recovery services, and exchange incidents.

Alert Types

What belongs here.

Open alerts
Severity Labels

High, medium, low.

High means active user-loss risk; medium means credible warning or exploit chain; low means education, hygiene, or emerging watch item.

Scam cases
Reader Action

Do not panic-click.

Pause, verify official domains, revoke suspicious permissions, move funds only from a clean device, and do not share private keys.
