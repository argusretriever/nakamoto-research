Should I?

Short answers before big decisions.

These pages help readers decide what to learn next. They are educational, not financial advice.

Should I buy Bitcoin?

Only after understanding volatility, custody, scams, fees, taxes, and your own risk limits. Start with the buying checklist.

Open checklist
Should I use a hardware wallet?

For meaningful savings, a hardware wallet is usually safer than a hot wallet, but only if the seed phrase is backed up correctly.

Compare wallets
Should I leave Bitcoin on Coinbase or another exchange?

Exchanges are useful for buying and selling. Long-term storage creates counterparty risk and should be understood clearly.

Read custody guide
Should I run a node?

Most beginners do not need one immediately. A node matters more when a user wants independent verification, privacy, or deeper technical control.

Read node guide
Should my business accept Bitcoin?

Only after reviewing accounting, tax, custody, payment operations, refunds, volatility, and who controls funds.
