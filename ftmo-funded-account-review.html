Funded Accounts

FTMO review: rules first, opportunity second.

Funded-account challenges can look attractive, but the real product is a strict rules environment. The reader's job is to understand targets, drawdowns, fees, allowed behavior, and failure risk before applying.

Bottom Line

FTMO may fit disciplined traders, not beginners chasing capital.

The right question is not "can I get funded?" The right question is whether your risk process can survive fixed rules, time pressure, drawdown limits, and emotional pressure without forcing bad trades.

Potential Fit

Rules-based traders.

Traders with documented strategy, risk per trade, review logs, and a history of respecting drawdown limits may have a reason to research FTMO.

Poor Fit

Beginners and impulsive traders.

If you are still learning structure, sizing, stops, journaling, and emotional control, a challenge fee can become tuition for the wrong lesson.

Check First

Objectives and restrictions.

Always read current FTMO rules directly: account type, profit target, loss limits, trading days, allowed instruments, payouts, and prohibited behavior.

Rules To Verify

The official rules matter more than the marketing page.

Profit Target

Know the target before sizing.

FTMO publishes profit target rules by challenge type. Do not assume every account has the same target or phase structure.

Maximum Daily Loss

Daily limits can end the attempt.

A trader can be right about direction and still fail by breaching daily loss rules or misunderstanding how limits are calculated.

Maximum Loss

Trailing and account-level limits matter.

Read whether loss limits are static, trailing, balance-based, equity-based, or reset under certain conditions.

Costs

Fees are real risk capital.

Only use money you can afford to lose. Challenge fees should never come from rent, bills, debt payments, or emergency funds.

Decision Checklist

Before opening FTMO, answer these without guessing.

Plan

Can you explain your setup, invalidation, position size, and maximum daily risk in writing?

History

Do you have a journal showing you followed rules for weeks or months, not only one lucky run?

Pressure

Can you stop trading after a bad day, even when the challenge fee makes you want to force recovery?

Rules

Have you read the current official FTMO objectives, terms, payout rules, and prohibited practices?
