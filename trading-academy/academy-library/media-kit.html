Approved talking points

NAKAMOTO Academy teaches trading as a curriculum, not a pile of random tactics. The platform uses visual diagrams to simplify hard market concepts. Practice tools help students think about risk, expectancy, and process. NAKAMOTO Academy is education only and does not provide trade signals.

Claims to avoid

Do not say NAKAMOTO Academy guarantees profit, predicts markets, provides personal investment advice, sells trade alerts, replaces risk management, or makes trading easy.

Do not use income screenshots, pressure language, fake scarcity, or performance claims. The creator message should feel like a school, not a casino advertisement.
