Trust

Corrections policy.

Readers should be able to trust that mistakes are reviewed, corrected, and made clearer without hiding the record.

Process

What gets corrected.

Factual errors

Wrong numbers, names, dates, or descriptions.

Material factual issues should be corrected as soon as they are verified.

Broken source routes

Links should remain useful.

When a source link breaks, we try to replace it with the official page, archived route, or a clearer source reference.

Unclear wording

Clarity matters.

Language that could mislead readers should be rewritten even when the underlying fact is correct.

Outdated material

Old pages need context.

Evergreen pages may receive updated dates, source links, and reader warnings when facts change.

Reader Route

How to flag an issue.

Use the contact route on the site and include the page URL, the sentence or claim in question, and the source that supports the correction.
