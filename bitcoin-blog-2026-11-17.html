Match the labour-market measure before comparing its direction

Published 18 November 2026

Practise comparing survey rates, payroll employment and vacancies using their own reference periods and revision status.

Retrospective archive slot 17 November 2026. Written on 18 November.

Build three source notes

  1. Record the unemployment rate, population coverage and July to September reference period.
  2. Record the early October payroll estimate and mark it provisional.
  3. Record the vacancy estimate and its August to October reference period.

Compare direction after defining each measure

The November release estimated unemployment at 4.6%, provisional October payroll employment at 30.2 million and vacancies at 690,000. These combine a survey rate, an administrative employee count and a count of unfilled jobs. Record each movement against the comparison period given in its own section of the release.

Check the explanation

The ONS said that differences between survey groups entering and leaving partly affected the quarterly survey movements. Add this qualification to your note, then explain why a falling unemployment rate can appear alongside weaker payroll and vacancy figures.
