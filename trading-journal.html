Template

A clean journal page should answer six questions.

This is the minimum evidence set for useful review. It keeps attention on decision quality instead of outcome noise.

01

Market state

Trend, range, transition, failed break, or news-driven volatility?

02

Thesis

What evidence made the idea worth studying?

03

Invalidation

Where is the read wrong, and why?

04

Risk

What was the planned risk, size, and maximum loss?

05

Behavior

Did fear, greed, boredom, revenge, or overconfidence appear?

06

Review note

What one rule or drill should improve before the next session?
