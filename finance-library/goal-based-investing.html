Goal framework

Goal-based investing knowledge

Each goal needs its own time horizon, risk level, funding plan, account type, and review rhythm.

Separate goals by horizon

Short goals need stability; long goals can usually accept more volatility.

Example: emergency fund and retirement portfolio should not share the same risk profile.

  • Mistake: one portfolio for every goal.
Match account to purpose

Tax treatment, access, and contribution rules should fit the goal.

Example: retirement accounts fit long-term retirement wealth, taxable accounts offer flexibility.

  • Mistake: ignoring withdrawal constraints.
Review funding rate

Goals fail when contributions are unrealistic or not automated.

Example: increase contribution rate after income changes.

  • Mistake: focusing only on returns.
