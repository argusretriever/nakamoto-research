Buyer Guide

How to choose a money app.

A money app should make cash flow easier to manage. Compare fees, FX costs, card features, transfer limits, protections, savings tools, and records before opening another account.

Steps

Use this order.

1

Choose the job.

Daily spending, travel, transfers, budgeting, saving, or business separation each needs different features.

2

Compare costs.

Plan fees, FX fees, ATM fees, transfer fees, card fees, and replacement-card costs.

3

Check protections.

Understand what is protected in your country and what is not.

4

Keep records.

Statements, transfers, fees, rewards, interest, and tax-relevant transactions should be easy to export.
