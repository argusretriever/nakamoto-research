FAQ

Simple Bitcoin answers.

Short answers for readers who want clarity before deeper guides.

Can Bitcoin be hacked?

The Bitcoin network has a strong security record, but wallets, exchanges, passwords, devices, and users can be compromised.

What if I lose my seed phrase?

If the wallet is lost and the seed phrase is gone, the bitcoin may be permanently unrecoverable.

Is Bitcoin anonymous?

No. Bitcoin is pseudonymous. Transactions are public and can sometimes be linked to real identities.

Can the government ban Bitcoin?

Governments can restrict exchanges, banks, mining, promotion, and tax treatment, but the protocol is globally distributed.

Is Bitcoin too late?

That depends on a reader's goals, risk tolerance, time horizon, and understanding. No page should promise returns.

Should I leave Bitcoin on an exchange?

Exchanges are useful for buying and selling. Long-term storage usually requires learning self-custody or using a qualified custodian.

How much Bitcoin should I buy?

This site does not give financial advice. Readers should only consider amounts they can afford to hold through volatility or lose.

What is the safest first step?

Learn the basics, use strong account security, avoid scams, and test small amounts before storing meaningful funds.
