ONS estimated August public borrowing at £18.3 billion

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-22. Original source published on this earlier date.

The September public-finances release estimated UK public sector borrowing of £18.3 billion in August 2026, £2.9 billion above a year earlier.

What the release reported

The ONS said spending rose faster than receipts, partly reflecting inflation. Financial-year borrowing to August was below the same period a year earlier and above the official forecast. Monthly borrowing is a flow, while debt measures an accumulated stock.

Account for revisions

The next bulletin lowered its estimate of financial-year borrowing to August by £4.0 billion to £73.3 billion. That revision covered the year-to-date period. Record the forecast publication, outturn release and financial-year window when comparing performance with forecasts.
