Why extra payments matter

If the payment is only slightly above monthly interest, payoff can move slowly. Increasing the payment can reduce the number of months and the total interest pressure.

Watch out

APR can change

Credit card promotional rates, penalty rates, fees, and new purchases can make real payoff different from the estimate.
