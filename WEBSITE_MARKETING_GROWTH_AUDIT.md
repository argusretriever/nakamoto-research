## 1. Executive Summary

NAKAMOTO is no longer a small static site. The crawl found 130 HTML pages, 6,762 internal references, 1,696 external references, 7 pages with forms, 49 calendar pages, 14 news pages, 10 daily operations pages, and a broad evergreen Bitcoin education library.

The strongest assets are topical breadth, daily publishing, source-first news behavior, rich internal linking, tools, archives, and a real editorial standards page. The biggest bottlenecks are conversion capture, content packaging, authority signals, page weight, monetization readiness, and public-file hygiene.

The site has no broken internal links or broken anchors in the local crawl. The larger growth problem is not broken navigation; it is that the site has more content than productized journeys. Visitors can browse, but the best paths, strongest proof, lead magnets, trust assets, and recurring reader loops need to be sharper.

## 3. Website Marketing Audit

### What Exists

- Clear Bitcoin/news/research positioning.
- Strong daily news and archive habit.
- Multiple evergreen education hubs.
- Source library and editorial standards page.
- Tools and calculators.
- Newsletter forms.
- Search interface.
- RSS feed and sitemap.
- Calendar pages for long-tail discovery.

### What Is Missing

- Stronger monetization architecture: sponsor page, advertise page, media kit, affiliate disclosure page.
- Productized lead magnets: PDF wallet checklist, scam checklist, ETF guide, RWA guide, tax checklist.
- Author/entity pages for EEAT.
- Methodology pages for news, source scoring, and tool calculations.
- Topic cluster landing pages for Bitcoin ETF, wallets, RWA, scams, stablecoins, mining, and regulation.
- Testimonials/reviews are not essential for a news site, but reader trust proof is missing: citations count, correction log, reader promise, public methodology.
- A real privacy policy and terms page.
- Conversion modules on most article pages.

### What Should Be Removed or Hidden

- Public access to internal Markdown/process files if not intended.
- Public access to PowerShell, deploy helper, and marketing stack files if deployed from repo root.
- Any raw extraction files or operational notes that are not meant for readers.

Business impact: reduces trust risk, keeps brand focused, prevents public confusion, and avoids exposing operational details.
