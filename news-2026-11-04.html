UK international reserves: what to check in the October release

Published 4 November 2026

The Bank of England scheduled its October reserves release for 4 November. The release page reviewed for this edition still showed a publication notice. Check the original page for the tables and any revisions before recording a figure.

Read the release in two steps

Record the reference month and the currency unit first. Then identify the specific reserve or liquidity measure before comparing it with the prior month. A difference between two reported balances can reflect valuation changes as well as transactions.

The Bank’s calendar separately schedules its policy summary and Monetary Policy Report for 5 November at noon London time.
