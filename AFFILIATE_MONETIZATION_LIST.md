## Link Placement Strategy

1. Primary placement: `trading-resources.html`.
2. Secondary placement: internal links from Trading Academy pages to `trading-resources.html`.
3. Future pages: honest comparison and review pages only after adding risk notes, pricing notes, pros and cons, who it is for, who should avoid it, and source links.
4. Do not place affiliate links in daily news unless the story directly requires a resource mention.
5. Wallet affiliate links belong only on wallet/custody pages such as `bitcoin-wallet-safety.html`, `resources.html#wallets`, or future wallet comparison pages, with clear disclosure and seed-phrase safety warnings.
6. Keep outbound partner links limited per page so the site does not feel spammy.
7. Exchange affiliate links belong on exchange education, exchange risk, spot trading tutorial, futures education, and crypto spending/card pages only. Do not use them as generic calls to action across unrelated pages.

## MEXC Placement Strategy

Use the specific MEXC link that matches the page intent:

1. Homepage referral link: use only on a dedicated MEXC resource or exchange overview page, not the main Nakamoto homepage.
2. Custom sign-up link: use on a future "How to Join MEXC" guide with jurisdiction, KYC, fees, withdrawal, and custody checks.
3. BTC/USDT spot link: use on spot trading tutorials after explaining order-book, fee, withdrawal, and custody risks.
4. BTC/USDT futures link: use only on futures education with leverage, liquidation, funding-rate, margin, and loss-risk warnings.
5. MEXC Card link: use only on a card review or spending-crypto guide with fees, tax, jurisdiction, and counterparty-risk notes.

MEXC risk requirements:

1. Label links as affiliate or sponsored where practical.
2. Use `rel="sponsored nofollow noreferrer"` on public pages.
3. Add a visible exchange-risk note before links.
4. Tell readers to check whether the product is available and lawful in their country.
5. Remind readers that exchange balances are custodial and can involve counterparty, withdrawal, compliance, and security risk.
6. Do not imply MEXC is the best exchange unless a full, source-checked comparison supports that claim.
