ONS updates its population statistics plans

Published 17 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 16 November 2026.

The ONS published a quarterly progress update and a population statistics system plan on 16 November. Readers checking population estimates should record the geography, reference date and release version. The update says the next provisional long-term migration release is due on 26 November and will cover the period up to June 2026.
