Retrospective exercise for 23 October 2026

Retail sales volume is not the same as spending

The ONS retail bulletin reports goods bought in volume terms, and it also distinguishes month-on-month from quarter-on-quarter comparisons.

Written and published 24 October 2026. Archive study date: 23 October 2026. This page does not claim it was available on the earlier date.

Find the unit

Original source. The ONS September 2026 retail-sales bulletin estimates that sales volumes fell 0.4% from August to September, while volumes across July–September rose 0.7% against April–June. Volume is an estimate of the quantity bought after adjusting for prices. It is not the same as the amount of money spent.

Check the revision

The bulletin revised August monthly volume growth to 0.7% from 0.5%, and July to a 0.4% fall from a 0.5% fall. Put the earlier and later estimates beside their publication dates. A revision changes the recorded comparison; it does not mean shoppers returned to August and made new purchases.

Choose the right question

For a business or investment thesis, decide whether you need real quantities, cash sales, margins, or one retailer’s own disclosures. The national volume series cannot supply all four. Never treat one monthly fall and one quarterly rise as contradictory without first writing their time windows.
