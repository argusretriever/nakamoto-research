Thesis discipline

Thesis library knowledge

A thesis library should collect ideas with evidence, risks, valuation, and update triggers so decisions remain auditable.

Write the bear case

A serious thesis includes what can go wrong.

Example: margin pressure, competition, debt, regulation, or valuation compression.

  • Mistake: writing only upside.
Define evidence

Use filings, cash flow, unit economics, balance sheet, and industry data.

Example: cite the 10-K rather than a headline.

  • Mistake: confusing opinion with evidence.
Track triggers

A thesis should say what changes the view.

Example: revenue deceleration below a threshold.

  • Mistake: holding because of sunk cost.
