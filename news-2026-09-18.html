August retail sales rose in the first ONS estimate

Published 18 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-09-18. Original source published on this earlier date.

The first ONS estimate put Great Britain’s August 2026 retail sales volumes up 0.5% over the month and 0.9% over the three months to August.

What the release reported

The monthly increase followed an estimated 0.5% July fall. Non-store retailers partly recovered and department stores also improved. The volume measure adjusts for price changes, while sales values measure the amount spent.

Preserve the release vintage

The September bulletin, published on 23 October, revised August’s monthly increase to 0.7%. When reconstructing a historical chart, record both the reference month and the release date. The first estimate’s three-month comparison covered June to August against March to May.
