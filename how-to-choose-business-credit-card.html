Buyer Guide

How to choose a business credit card.

A business card can help operations or damage cash flow. Start with repayment discipline, then compare fees, rewards, controls, records, and eligibility.

Steps

Use this order.

1

Check repayment capacity.

If the business cannot clear balances reliably, rewards are not the priority.

2

Compare real cost.

Interest, annual fees, late fees, FX fees, cash advance rules, and repayment dates.

3

Review controls.

Employee cards, limits, approvals, receipt capture, categories, and exports.

4

Track records.

Statements, receipts, fees, interest, rewards, and tax/accounting evidence.
