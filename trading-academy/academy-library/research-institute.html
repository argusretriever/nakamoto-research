Survey Framework

  1. Consent and education-only boundary.
  2. Experience level and market exposure.
  3. Term confidence rating.
  4. Scenario questions.

Fact-Checking Framework

  1. Tag every claim as definition, process, historical, statistical, or opinion.
  2. Require official or high-quality source for factual claims.
  3. Reject profit guarantees and unsupported predictive claims.
  4. Date source reviews.
  5. Publish correction notes.
