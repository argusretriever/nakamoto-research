Editorial policy.

NAKAMOTO is built for source-first Bitcoin and crypto research. We separate news, analysis, education, and Promotion so readers can understand what they are reading.

Standards

How we publish.

Source first

Important claims need a route back to evidence.

We prefer official documents, regulator pages, company releases, filings, primary data, and clearly attributed reporting.

No hype

Headlines should explain, not manipulate.

We avoid guaranteed-outcome language, fake urgency, price promises, and misleading clickbait.

Reader safety

Risk context belongs beside opportunity.

Wallet, custody, ETF, RWA, stablecoin, and trading stories should include practical risks when relevant.

Education only

No financial advice.

NAKAMOTO does not provide investment, legal, tax, or trading advice.

Promotion separation

Ads do not set editorial conclusions.

Promotion and analytics may support the site, but editorial pages should remain useful and clearly written for readers.

Corrections

Errors should be fixed clearly.

Material corrections should be updated quickly and routed through the corrections policy.
