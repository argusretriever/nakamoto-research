Seven-day launch

A first week that actually moves.

The sequence is intentionally narrow. A new student should learn the map, finish one lesson, practice one small decision, and review the result before going deeper.

Day 1Take assessment

Score current foundations, risk, execution, and review.

Day 2Open first lesson

Study markets, orders, or structure depending on your path.

Day 3Visualize

Use one diagram or animated explainer to see the concept.

Day 4Practice

Complete one chart drill, scenario, or calculator task.

Day 5Journal

Write one observation, mistake, question, or rule.

Day 6Review

Use the performance review lab or progress wall.

Day 7Choose next

Move into lessons, challenges, cohort, or rescue path.
