Method

How daily updates work.

Fresh stories should update Crypto News Today. Before replacing that page, save the old version as a dated file such as news-YYYY-MM-DD.html, add it to this archive, and add the headlines to news-posted-log.md so the same stories are not reposted without a new development. The full ranking method lives in the Bitcoin Intelligence Center.

Priority 1

Bitcoin first.

Bitcoin price, ETF activity, mining, network security, custody, and public-company adoption get the highest weight.

Priority 2

Major networks only.

Ethereum, Solana, and XRP are included when the story affects liquidity, regulation, security, or institutional access.

Priority 3

Policy and risk.
