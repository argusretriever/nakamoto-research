Terms of Use

Use NAKAMOTO Finance Library as an educational research platform. Do not treat content, tools, or examples as personalized financial advice.

Educational contentArticles, tools, dashboards, calculators, academies, simulations, and research formats are for learning and decision support.
No personal adviceNAKAMOTO Finance Library does not know your complete financial situation and does not provide individualized recommendations.
Tool limitationsCalculators and simulators use simplified assumptions. They should support questions and planning, not guarantee outcomes.
Responsible useInvesting and trading involve risk. Users are responsible for validating information and consulting qualified professionals where appropriate.
