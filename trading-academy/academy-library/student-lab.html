Student experience system

Learn trading through paths, drills, quizzes, and visual memory systems.

This lab turns the curriculum into student actions: learn the idea, see the model, mark the chart, check recall, review mistakes, and move to the next lesson.

Beginner Path

Financial markets, asset classes, bid and ask, orders, spread, liquidity, volatility, risk, and basic market structure.

  • Output: market mechanics checklist
  • Quiz: order type scenarios
  • Assignment: annotate basic trend structure

Intermediate Path

Technical analysis, support and resistance, price action, auction market theory, volume profile, and market profile.

  • Output: chart annotation deck
  • Quiz: structure and profile terms
  • Assignment: balance vs imbalance case study

Advanced Path

Order flow, delta, footprint charts, DOM, liquidity, ICT, SMC, quant testing, risk models, and professional review.

  • Output: process journal
  • Quiz: risk and context
  • Assignment: full trade thesis post-mortem

Built learning assets

Lessons, assignments, quizzes, and retention loops.

NAKAMOTO Academy teaches trading as education, not signals. Every activity protects the learner from confusing chart reading with prediction.

Lesson Template

Definition: plain English concept. Context: when it appears in markets. Visual: diagram or chart model. Practice: annotation drill. Risk: failure case. Review: recall quiz.

Assignments

  1. Mark HH, HL, LH, LL on 20 charts.
  2. Label support/resistance zones and invalidations.
  3. Compare balanced and imbalanced auction days.
  4. Write a risk-first trade thesis without an entry signal.

Quiz Bank

  1. What does spread measure?
  2. How is liquidity different from volume?
  3. What confirms a break of structure?
  4. Why can a level fail after multiple tests?
  5. What is the purpose of a stop loss?

Retention System

Day 0: learn. Day 1: recall terms. Day 3: annotate a chart. Day 7: explain the concept from memory. Day 14: compare examples. Day 30: review mistakes and update checklist.
