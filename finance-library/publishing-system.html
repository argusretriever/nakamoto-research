Daily production model

Ten pieces a day without turning the library into noise.

2 daily briefsMarket open and closing recap linked to Market Pulse, Economic Calendar, News, and Archives.
2 analysis notesOne market or economic insight plus one sector, company, ETF, or portfolio observation.
2 evergreen guidesFoundational articles mapped to Learn, Glossary, Investing, ETFs, Stocks, or Portfolios.
1 research reportStructured stock, ETF, sector, thesis, case study, or long-form report with related tools.
1 academy lessonInvestor Academy or Trading Academy module with progress, prerequisites, and next steps.
1 tool/resource updateCalculator, checklist, worksheet, glossary entry, watchlist prompt, or resource library item.
1 newsletter/archive assetDaily brief, weekly report, monthly recap, or curated collection that keeps older work discoverable.
Everyday operations

The daily list keeps new work fresh and old work visible.

This checklist should run every publishing day. It adds fresh value without deleting, hiding, or replacing previous NAKAMOTO Finance Library articles, guides, reports, tools, or archives.

Publish 10 fresh updatesEach item needs a headline, short summary, source link, why it matters, and investor takeaway.
Update the daily briefingMake today's headlines visible on the homepage, News hub, Market Pulse, archives, search, and newsletter paths.
Keep source links attachedUse official sources such as BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve, Treasury, SEC EDGAR, FRED, company filings, ETF issuers, and Investor.gov.
Add one evergreen improvementImprove or publish one durable guide, glossary entry, calculator note, checklist, academy lesson, or portfolio resource.
Refresh Market PulseUpdate valuation, rates, breadth, earnings, risk, liquidity, and the practical action note.
Add related linksEvery new item should connect to an article, category hub, tool, glossary term, report, academy path, archive, or newsletter.
Preserve older contentDo not remove old work. Keep it reachable through archives, categories, search, topic pages, related content, and historical context.
Prepare newsletter outputTurn the strongest daily updates into a daily brief, weekly recap, or saved reading path.
Run quality checksCheck mobile layout, links, buttons, empty cards, accessibility labels, source visibility, and whether each section has useful information.
Maintain the pipelineTrack planned, drafted, reviewed, published, updated, archived, and refreshed items so daily work does not get lost.

Publishing operations

Publishing system knowledge

A high-volume investing platform needs a daily content system that protects quality, sourcing, and internal linking.

Ten pieces need roles

Daily output should mix news, glossary, evergreen updates, tools, and research.

Example: three headlines, one analysis, one glossary, one tool update.

  • Mistake: publishing ten shallow articles.
Every article needs a trail

Content should link to sources, glossary, related pages, and next actions.

Example: CPI article links to BLS, FOMC, market pulse, and portfolio guide.

  • Mistake: dead-end pages.
Review old content

Archives must stay useful as facts change.

Example: update evergreen guides quarterly.

  • Mistake: letting old pages rot.
