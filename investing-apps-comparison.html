Comparison Framework

Choose by the investment job.

ETF investor

Funds and allocation.

Check fund range, ETF costs, platform fees, rebalancing workflow, tax wrapper support, and portfolio exports.

Stock picker

Single-share research.

Check market access, dealing costs, FX fees, fractional shares, order types, statements, and risk warnings.

Beginner

Education and friction.

Good apps make fees, risks, account type, tax records, and investment limits understandable before trading.

Long-term

Behavior matters.

Watch whether the app encourages overtrading, speculation, reward chasing, or poor diversification.

Product Table

Investing-app decision table.

Best for

Long-term investing workflow.

Investors who need accounts, funds, statements, tax records, and low-friction portfolio review.

Key risks

Capital loss and overtrading.

Apps can encourage activity. Investments can fall and rewards should not drive product choice.

Fees to verify

Platform, fund, trading, FX.

Check platform fees, fund costs, dealing fees, spreads, FX, subscriptions, and transfer charges.

Records needed

Tax and transaction records.

Keep statements, contract notes, dividends, interest, fees, transfers, and capital gains records.

Avoid if

You do not know the plan.

Do not open an app because of a free-share offer without an asset allocation and risk plan.
