Reader safety

If a wallet, seed phrase, exchange account, or device may be compromised, stop sending funds, disconnect approvals, preserve evidence, and use official support channels only. Nakamoto does not recover funds.

Incident Template

Every security entry should answer these questions.

Attack

What happened?

Phishing, fake wallet, malware, bridge exploit, exchange incident, approval drain, SIM swap, or recovery scam.

Target

Who was exposed?

Retail wallets, exchange users, protocol users, developers, treasury operators, or social-media followers.

Vector

How did it spread?

Search ads, fake websites, Discord links, email, browser extension, app store, token approval, or compromised account.

Proof

What source confirms it?

Official incident report, regulator alert, security advisory, on-chain evidence, or company statement.

Action

What should readers do?

Revoke approvals, move funds, change passwords, rotate devices, verify URLs, or avoid fake support.

Lesson

What becomes evergreen?

Turn each incident into a checklist, warning label, guide update, or tool improvement.
