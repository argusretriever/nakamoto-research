# Nakamoto Premium Pricing Research Note

Internal only. Do not publish as a website page.

## Proposed Starting Ranges

1. Starter: GBP 5-9/month or GBP 59-89/year.
2. Nakamoto Pro: GBP 15-29/month or GBP 150-290/year.
3. Research Plus: GBP 39-99/month or GBP 390-990/year.

## Positioning Logic

Starter is the habit tier. Pro is the main product. Research Plus is for advanced readers who want deeper reports, dashboards, source databases, and topic influence.

## Test Before Final Pricing

1. Track waitlist clicks by tier.
2. Track selected topics.
3. Ask readers what they expected to receive.
4. Prepare first-month content before charging.
5. Avoid overpricing before trust, delivery, and retention are proven.

## Do Not Do

1. Do not launch pricing before refund terms exist.
2. Do not promise returns.
3. Do not sell signals.
4. Do not hide cancellation details.
5. Do not make free content useless.
