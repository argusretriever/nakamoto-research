ONS tests household size estimates built from administrative data

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 11 November 2026.

The ONS compared administrative estimates of household size with Census 2021 for England and Wales.

How to read the release

For two- and three-person households, the combined England and Wales estimates were within 0.5% of the census figures. Differences varied for other sizes and across regions. The ONS classifies these figures as official statistics in development and publishes them for comparison with existing estimates.
