Level 2 course

Auction Theory: price as a discovery process.

This course connects market structure to auction behavior: balance, imbalance, acceptance, rejection, value, volume profile, and market profile.

Level 24 modulesVisual courseQuiz included

Course modules

The auction map.

Students should learn that markets test prices. The chart is the visible record of acceptance, rejection, and value migration.

01

Price Discovery

Markets search for prices where two-sided trade can occur. Discovery happens through movement, reaction, participation, and failure.

02

Balance and Imbalance

Balance means two-sided trade is accepted. Imbalance means one side forces price away from the prior area.

03

Acceptance and Rejection

A breakout matters less than what happens after it. Does price hold, build value, and invite trade, or snap back?

04

Volume and Market Profile

Profile turns trading activity into structure: POC, VAH, VAL, HVN, LVN, initial balance, and value migration.

Visual model

From range to discovery.

When price rotates inside a range, the auction is balanced. When price leaves and accepts outside, the auction is discovering new value. When it fails back inside, the break may have been rejection or a liquidity event.
