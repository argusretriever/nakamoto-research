Four Numbers

Every learner should know these before risking capital.

Expectancy

Average result per trade.

Win rate alone is useless without average win and average loss.

Recovery

Losses need larger gains to recover.

A 20% drawdown needs a 25% gain to return to breakeven.

Risk pressure

Sizing controls survival.

A decent idea can still fail if risk per trade is too high.

Position size

Translate plan into exposure.

Size comes after stop distance and risk limit, not before.

Worked Examples

Use these examples as a study checklist.

Expectancy

45% wins, +1.8R wins, -1R losses.

Expected result: (0.45 x 1.8) - (0.55 x 1.0) = +0.26R before costs and slippage.

Drawdown

12% loss needs about 13.6% recovery.

Recovery return = drawdown divided by remaining capital. The deeper the hole, the harder the climb.

Position Size

$10,000 account, 1% risk, $50 stop.

Risk budget is $100. If each unit risks $50, maximum educational size is 2 units before costs.

Risk Rule

Pause when behavior breaks.

If a learner moves stops, revenge trades, or ignores invalidation, the plan has already failed.
