What the November Bank of England scenarios assume

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 8 November 2026.

The Bank of England’s November report examines how persistent energy costs and weaker demand could change the inflation outlook.

How to read the release

The adverse scenario explores persistent energy costs and stronger effects on wages and prices. The weaker-demand scenario examines how spare capacity could limit those effects. Read the assumptions behind each path before comparing the projections.
