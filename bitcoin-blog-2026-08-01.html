Mining averages are not a daily schedule

Separate a long-run expectation from a guaranteed sequence.

Archive slot: 1 August 2026

A worked example

In a deliberately simplified classroom game, a participant has one chance in ten of success per round. Ten rounds can still produce no success or several successes. Drawing the expected one win as a promised event in round ten would misrepresent the game.

What to take from it

An average is not a timetable for individual outcomes.
