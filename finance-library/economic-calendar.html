Calendar literacy

Economic calendar knowledge

Economic events matter when they update inflation, growth, employment, rates, or earnings assumptions.

Know the release

Understand what CPI, PCE, payrolls, GDP, and FOMC actually measure.

Example: CPI and PCE are related but not identical.

  • Mistake: reacting without reading definitions.
Watch revisions

Initial releases can change.

Example: GDP revisions can alter the growth story.

  • Mistake: treating first print as final truth.
Connect to portfolio areas

Each event maps to cash, bonds, equities, sectors, or spending assumptions.

Example: payroll strength can affect rate expectations.

  • Mistake: assuming every event matters equally.
