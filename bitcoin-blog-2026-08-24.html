Separate a price level from its growth rate

Read the units before interpreting a headline.

Archive slot: 24 August 2026
Published 13 September 2026. This educational retrospective fills an archive gap. It is not a report of market conditions on the archive date.

Source foundation

CPI measures changes in consumer prices; it is not an individual household budget. BLS: CPI questions and answers.

A worked example

A made-up price index rises from 100 to 105 and then to 108. The second increase is smaller in percentage terms: 3 divided by 105 is about 2.86 percent, compared with 5 percent initially. Yet the index level is higher at the end of both periods.

Original hypothetical example. Amounts, people, events, and scenarios above are invented for learning; they are not historical observations or forecasts.

What to take from it

Slower price growth does not mean the price level has fallen.
