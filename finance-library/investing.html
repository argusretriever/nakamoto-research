Investing foundation

The long-term investing operating system

Long-term investing works best when goals, allocation, behavior, taxes, and review cadence are written before markets get emotional.

Start with goals

Define time horizon, cash needs, contribution plan, and acceptable drawdown before picking securities.

Example: retirement in 30 years and home purchase in 3 years should not use the same portfolio.

  • Mistake: buying popular assets before defining purpose.
Automate what should repeat

Contributions, review dates, rebalancing rules, and learning routines should not depend on mood.

Example: monthly contribution and quarterly review.

  • Mistake: checking daily but reviewing never.
Measure behavior, not just returns

A good plan must survive volatility, boredom, and headlines.

Example: use an investment policy statement.

  • Mistake: judging a process by one month of returns.
