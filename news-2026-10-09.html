ONS expands its explanation of trade revisions

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 9 October 2026

The October update explains how the ONS reports revisions and uncertainty in services trade. The bulletin now discusses which periods and categories changed and why. A rolling three-month series can help when monthly estimates are volatile.

How to use the release

Save the release date and compare the same period in two editions. Check whether a difference comes from new observations, balancing adjustments or a change in method.
