Separate reserve stocks, transactions and valuation changes

Published 4 November 2026

Build a worksheet that distinguishes a change in reported reserves from a transaction.

Work the example

Suppose a fictional balance rises from 100 to 103. A valuation gain of 5 and net transactions of −2 would explain the same change. Label each component, then leave the breakdown blank if the release does not provide enough evidence.

Record the source table, reference month, units, comparison period and any revisions. Use the evidence worksheet to keep each field separate.
