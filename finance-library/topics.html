Internal linking

Every topic should create useful paths.

A scalable publication links horizontally across context and vertically from beginner explanations to advanced research.

Article to hubEvery new post links to its category hub, topic directory, archive, author desk, and related newsletter.
Article to toolGuides connect to calculators, portfolio simulator, ETF comparison, watchlist, alerts, or Portfolio Clinic.
Article to knowledgeTechnical terms link to glossary, investing wiki, academy lesson, and evergreen explainer.
Article to journeyBeginner content routes to Investor Academy; advanced research routes to Research Lab, reports, and thesis library.
Article to historyMarket news links to market history, weekly reports, monthly recaps, and archived context.
Topic architecture

Topic hubs create knowledge clusters

A topic page should gather definitions, guides, tools, articles, sources, and next steps around one investor question.

Cluster by intent

Group content by what the reader wants to do.

Example: learn ETFs, compare ETFs, build ETF portfolio.

  • Mistake: tags without purpose.
Link across formats

Topics should include articles, glossary, tools, templates, and reports.

Example: rebalancing topic links guide, calculator, template, and case study.

  • Mistake: one content type only.
Support SEO and AI search

Clear topic hubs help search engines and AI systems understand authority.

Example: concise answer plus deeper resources.

  • Mistake: thin tag pages.
