Learning paths

Choose the path that matches the problem, not the loudest topic.

Each path has a purpose, prerequisites, first lesson, first practice action, and next system. This is the student map that should sit behind every page.

Path 01

Beginner Foundations

For students who do not understand market language, orders, assets, or risk. Start with markets and trading basics before charts.

Path 02

Chart Reading

For learners who know basic terms but cannot read structure, levels, trends, candle reactions, or invalidation clearly.

For students who understand concepts but break rules, size poorly, revenge trade, or cannot review performance honestly.

Use these short study cards before deeper lessons. They explain what the thing is, why it matters, and what to practice first.

Beginner

What is a market?

A market is a place where buyers and sellers agree on price. Your job as a beginner is to notice where agreement holds and where it fails.

Read full lesson
Example

Bid and ask

The bid is what buyers offer. The ask is what sellers want. The spread is the gap. Wide spreads usually mean execution is harder.

Study mechanics
Chart

Market structure

If price keeps making higher highs and higher lows, buyers are still making progress. If that sequence breaks, context may be changing.

Study structure
Risk

Risk per trade

Risk is the amount you can lose if the idea is wrong. Beginners should learn sizing before learning more setups.
