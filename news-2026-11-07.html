November decision: Bank Rate rises to 4%

Published 7 November 2026

A 7 November review of the Bank of England decision published on 5 November.

The published result

The MPC raised Bank Rate by 0.25 percentage points to 4%. Eight members supported that increase; one preferred a 0.5-point increase to 4.25%.

What the vote tells readers

The disagreement concerned the size of the increase. Recording only “8–1” loses that distinction. The decision is an observed policy action; an outlook is conditional analysis.

How to use this review

Record the announcement date, rate level, change and alternative vote separately. Compare a mortgage or savings product using its own terms and repricing date rather than assuming immediate transmission. This review does not establish a direction for Bitcoin or other asset prices.
