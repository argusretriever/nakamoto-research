Build a clean Canadian record trail.

Use this route to organize CRA-facing records, registered-account questions, crypto activity, property costs, and official regulator checks.

Checklist

What Canadian readers should organize.

Tax

Start with CRA guidance.

Check current official pages for reporting, crypto records, income, gains, losses, and filing responsibilities.

Investing

Understand account wrapper rules.

Separate account type, contribution records, holdings, fund fees, risk, and tax treatment questions.

Bitcoin

Preserve crypto records.

Keep trade exports, wallet histories, fees, transfers, staking or income notes, and disposal evidence.

Property

Stress-test total housing cost.

Include mortgage payments, rates, insurance, maintenance, taxes, strata or condo fees, and moving costs.

Business

Document revenue and expenses.

Keep invoices, receipts, bank exports, contracts, software records, and GST/HST questions if relevant.

Sources

Use official sources first.

Prioritize CRA, provincial regulators, Bank of Canada, securities regulators, and provider documents.
