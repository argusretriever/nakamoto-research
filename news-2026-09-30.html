ONS revised second-quarter UK GDP growth to 0.5%

Published 17 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 30 September 2026.

In its 30 September quarterly national accounts, the ONS revised UK real GDP growth for April to June 2026 from 0.4% to 0.5%. The preceding quarter remained at 0.6%.
