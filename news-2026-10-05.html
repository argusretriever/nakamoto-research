How to read the September UK reserves workbook

Published 15 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 5 October 2026

The Bank of England published its September reserves workbook on 5 October. The release covers foreign currencies, gold and reserve assets held through the IMF.

How to read the release

Use the IMF template headings to distinguish reserve holdings from foreign currency liquidity. Record the unit and reference month before comparing with the October workbook.
