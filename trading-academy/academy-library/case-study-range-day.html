Case study

Range day: when the best trade is often no chase.

Range days teach balance, patience, and location. Price rotates between accepted areas until new information creates imbalance.

Balance Map

Range highRange low

Decision rules

  • Trade location, not emotion.
  • Do not chase into the middle.
  • Define invalidation outside the range.
  • Wait for acceptance before calling breakout.

Beginner mistake

Assuming every move away from the middle is a trend. In balance, movement often rotates until proven otherwise.

Practice task

Mark high, low, midpoint, failed breaks, and no-trade middle zone on five historical range examples.

Review question

Did the trade start at an edge, or did it start from boredom inside the middle?
