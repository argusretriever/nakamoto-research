Learning path

Seven checks for a stronger claim

Work through these in order when a headline, chart, or market story appears persuasive.

01 · Source

Find the original.

Record the publisher, publication date, and exact claim.

02 · Data

Understand the measure.

Check definitions, units, coverage, and missing observations.

03 · Uncertainty

Test the comparison.

Consider sample size, base rates, correlation, and noise.

04 · Risk

Identify the downside.

Set boundaries before interpreting an opportunity.

05 · History

Use context carefully.

Compare mechanisms without assuming an old outcome will repeat.

06 · World

Check external forces.

Policy, trade, energy, and institutions can change the path.

07 · Practice

Put numbers beside it.

Use a worksheet or calculator to expose assumptions.
