Market Scenario Trainer

Practice the decision before the market makes it emotional.

Choose a scenario, read the context, check the evidence, and decide whether to trade, wait, or reduce size. The trainer grades the decision process, not prediction.

Start Scenario Review Structure

Read context

Each scenario starts with market state, location, risk condition, and what the chart is testing.

Check evidence

Students must confirm structure, levels, liquidity, invalidation, and risk before selecting an action.

Get feedback

The result explains why the decision fits or fails, then points the student back to the right practice tool.

Decision practice

Train process under chart pressure.

Use this like a rehearsal: identify the situation, collect enough evidence, then choose the safest professional action.

Trend pullback at prior resistance

Student use

Complete one scenario, write the reason in the journal, then replay the same logic on the chart simulator.

Coach use

Use the answers to see whether a learner is chasing entries, ignoring invalidation, or trading without enough evidence.

Business use

This becomes a repeat-visit training loop: new weekly scenarios, saved scores, and premium decision drills later.
