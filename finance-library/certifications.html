Certification knowledge

Certifications should prove skill through output

An investor certification should require artifacts: thesis, portfolio policy, checklist, journal, and source-backed explanation.

Assess application

Knowledge matters when it can be used.

Example: create an ETF comparison from real source documents.

  • Mistake: only multiple-choice recall.
Require sources

Submissions should cite official or high-authority references.

Example: SEC filings, issuer documents, economic releases.

  • Mistake: unsourced claims.
Score process

Grade clarity, risk framing, evidence, and next steps.

Example: a good answer explains limitations.

  • Mistake: rewarding confident predictions.
