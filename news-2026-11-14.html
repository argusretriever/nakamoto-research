UK short-term lets recorded 104.6 million guest nights

Published 14 November 2026

The ONS counted 104.6 million guest nights in UK short-term lets booked through three platforms in the year to June 2026, 10.2% more than the preceding year.

Reading the release

A guest night counts each person for each night. A party of three staying two nights contributes six guest nights. The bulletin covers rentals booked through Airbnb, Booking.com and Expedia Group. Hotels and campsites are outside this series. Use the local tables for geographic comparisons.
