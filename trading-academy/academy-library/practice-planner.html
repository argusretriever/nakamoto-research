Weekly planner

The output is intentionally practical: one focus, one task, one tool, and one review action per day.

How to use the plan

Do not add more tasks until the week is complete. Finish the small plan, review the evidence, then generate the next week.

What to track

Track completion, one mistake, one chart observation, and one risk rule. The goal is consistency, not intensity.
