UK real GDP grew 0.7% in the third quarter, ONS estimates

Published 12 November 2026

The ONS first estimate puts UK real GDP growth at 0.7% in July to September 2026 compared with the previous quarter.

How to read the release

Services output rose 0.9%, production rose 0.1% and construction fell 0.7%. The separate monthly estimate shows GDP growing 0.2% in September. August growth was revised from 0.1% to zero. Record the comparison period with each number and revisit the estimate when the ONS publishes revisions.

What the trade release adds

The ONS separately estimates that the goods and services trade deficit widened by £6.2 billion to £10.4 billion in the third quarter, excluding precious metals. This balance is measured in current prices. Compare it with the trade tables in the national accounts before drawing conclusions about real GDP growth.
