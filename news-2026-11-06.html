Public service productivity rose 0.3% on the ONS four-quarter measure

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 6 November 2026.

The ONS estimated a 0.3% rise in total public service productivity over the four quarters to June 2026 compared with the preceding four quarters.

How to read the release

The release is a developing statistical series. Its provisional annual estimate for 2025 was revised from 0.9% growth to 0.5%. Keep the calendar-year estimate and the rolling comparison in separate rows, with their own dates and vintages.
