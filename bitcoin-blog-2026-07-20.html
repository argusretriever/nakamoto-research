Write a recovery plan before an emergency

Describe recovery as a sequence that another authorized person could understand.

Archive slot: 20 July 2026
Published 13 September 2026. This educational retrospective fills an archive gap. It is not a report of market conditions on the archive date.

Source foundation

Wallet security includes backups, access protection, and recovery planning. Bitcoin: securing your wallet.

A worked example

Consider a fictional household whose wallet device is lost during a move. Its planning worksheet has three boxes: locate the protected backup, identify the legitimate recovery instructions, and confirm the restored wallet using a small test balance. The worksheet contains locations and responsibilities, never actual recovery secrets.

Original hypothetical example. Amounts, people, events, and scenarios above are invented for learning; they are not historical observations or forecasts.

What to take from it

A plan is useful only if its assumptions can be tested safely.

Try it on paper

Draft the sequence using an empty practice wallet and no real funds.

Keep the assumptions beside your answer. Check the result before applying the idea to a different situation.
