Privacy

Privacy Policy.

NAKAMOTO is an educational research website. This policy explains what data may be collected, why it is used, and how readers can control common browser, analytics, cookie, and Promotion choices.

Last updated

June 26, 2026

Data We Collect

We may collect basic technical data such as pages visited, device type, browser type, approximate location, referring pages, timestamps, and interactions with navigation, forms, links, or tools. If you submit a contact, newsletter, registration, or login-request form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as an email address, display name, topic preference, page URL, or message.

How We Use Data

We use data to operate the website, understand what readers find useful, improve page quality, detect broken experiences, protect forms from spam, measure content performance, and improve educational routes across Bitcoin, Trading Academy, Investing, and Personal Finance.

Google Services

The site may use Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google publisher Promotion, Google Ad Manager features, or related Google measurement and Promotion services. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, serve ads, limit repeated ads, detect invalid activity, and personalize or contextualize Promotion depending on user settings and applicable law.

Microsoft Services

The site may use Microsoft Clarity, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Microsoft Promotion/UET. These services can collect interaction, performance, and conversion information to help understand site usability and Promotion performance.

Cookies

Cookies and local storage may be used for theme preference, analytics, Promotion, fraud prevention, form behavior, and privacy notice preferences. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Some features may work less smoothly if storage is disabled.

Promotion

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Readers can manage Google ad personalization through Google ad settings and can use browser controls or regional consent tools where available.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information directly to readers or data brokers. Data may be processed by analytics, Promotion, hosting, form, security, and infrastructure providers that help run the website. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the site from abuse.

Reader Choices

You can use browser settings to limit cookies, use privacy tools, opt out of ad personalization where available, or avoid submitting forms. Contact requests can be sent through the contact page if you want to ask about data connected to a message you sent.

Reader Accounts

Reader account and login-request forms are used to prepare account access and preferences. Do not submit passwords, seed phrases, private keys, wallet details, exchange login details, financial account numbers, or sensitive identity documents through any NAKAMOTO form.

Saved Articles

The reader dashboard can save articles and topic preferences in your browser using local storage. These local saved items are not a secure account database and may disappear if you clear browser data or use another device.

Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or data requests, use the contact page and include the relevant page URL or form submission context.
