Interest rates FAQ.

Simple answers about central banks, bond yields, mortgages, savings, investing, business borrowing, and Bitcoin risk appetite.

Q

Why do interest rates matter?

Rates affect savings, borrowing, mortgages, business costs, asset valuations, and investor risk appetite.

Q

Do higher rates always hurt markets?

Not always, but higher rates can pressure valuations and reduce liquidity. Market reactions also depend on growth, inflation, earnings, and expectations.

Q

How do rates affect Bitcoin?

Rates can influence liquidity and risk appetite, which may affect Bitcoin demand even when long-term narratives remain unchanged.
