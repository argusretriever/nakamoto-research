Distinguish business births, deaths and survival rates

Published 20 November 2026

Use the annual business-demography release to practise choosing a denominator and a comparison cohort.

Start with the definitions

  1. Record the reference year and the release date.
  2. Read how the ONS defines a business birth and a business death.
  3. Identify the population used to calculate each rate.
  4. For survival, write down the starting cohort and the number of years followed.

Compare like with like

A one-year birth rate and the survival of an earlier cohort describe different processes. Compare the same cohort horizon, industry and geography before interpreting a difference.

Write a reproducible note

Save the dataset edition, series label and any caveat about revisions. Explain which part of the business population your comparison covers and how a later release could alter it.
