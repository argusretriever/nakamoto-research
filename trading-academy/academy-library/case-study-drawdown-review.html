Drawdown review: recover process before trying to recover money.

A drawdown is not only a loss number. It is evidence about risk sizing, setup quality, emotional behavior, and review discipline.

Pause trigger

Stop live trading after max daily loss, three rule breaks, or emotional state above your risk threshold.

Rebuild trigger

Return only after ten reviewed simulated trades following the written rules.

Learning task

Tag every losing trade by cause: valid loss, late entry, oversized risk, emotional decision, or unclear setup.
