What can a central bank settlement experiment establish?

Published 16 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 2026-11-15

The Bank of England published its Meridian FX APAC policy report on 13 November. The experiment explored how to coordinate linked currency payments across central bank test systems. The policy analysis distinguishes whether the prototype coordinates payments from when each payment becomes legally final. The report examines questions for a future service using findings from test environments.
