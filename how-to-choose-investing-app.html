Buyer Guide

How to choose an investing app.

The app is only the wrapper. The real decision is whether the account type, fees, fund range, records, and behavior design support your investing plan.

Steps

Use this order.

1

Choose account type.

General account, ISA, SIPP, pension, or country-specific wrapper changes tax and record needs.

2

Compare total cost.

Platform fees, fund costs, trading fees, FX fees, spreads, and transfer charges all matter.

3

Check investment range.

Make sure the app supports the funds, ETFs, shares, or strategy you actually need.

4

Review behavior risk.

A useful app should support discipline, not push overtrading or reward chasing.
