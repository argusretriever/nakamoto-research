Entrepreneurship Guide

Validate the business before you build the business.

The goal is not to prove your idea is exciting. The goal is to find evidence that a specific customer has a painful problem and will take action to solve it.

ChecklistTemplatesBreak-even guide

Validation Checklist

Look for behaviour, not compliments.

Customer

Who has the problem?

Write one specific audience, their current situation, what they tried already, and what makes the problem urgent.

Pain

What is the costly problem?

List time lost, money lost, stress, risk, missed opportunity, or repeated frustration.

Proof

What action proves demand?

Look for email signups, deposits, pre-orders, booked calls, repeated usage, referrals, or paid pilots.

Offer

What will they buy first?

Define the smallest valuable version: service, guide, audit, template, consultation, tool, or productized offer.

Validation Process

Run one small experiment at a time.

1

Interview 10 people.

Ask about their last real attempt to solve the problem. Avoid pitching too early.

2

Write a landing page.

Explain the painful problem, promise, proof, delivery, and who it is not for.

3

Ask for a commitment.

A reply, call, deposit, signup, or pilot is stronger than a friendly opinion.
