Definition

What does RWA mean in crypto?

RWA means real-world asset. In crypto, it usually means a token or on-chain record connected to a claim on something outside the blockchain.

Assets

What can be tokenized?

Treasurys, money-market funds, private credit, real estate funds, invoices, commodities, carbon credits, stocks, bonds, and other financial claims can be represented through token arrangements.

Claim

The token is not enough.

The key question is what the token legally represents. It might be a fund share, a receipt, a debt claim, a record of ownership, or only a platform balance.

Settlement

Blockchains do not remove every intermediary.

Issuers, custodians, transfer agents, administrators, banks, oracles, exchanges, and legal contracts can still sit behind the token.

Due Diligence

Seven checks before trusting an RWA token.

Issuer

Who issued it, where are they regulated, and what documents control the claim?

Custody

Who holds the asset, cash, securities, or collateral?

Redemption

Can holders redeem, who is eligible, and what happens during stress?

Liquidity

Is there a real market or only a dashboard number?

Jurisdiction

Which country, court, regulator, and investor rules apply?

Fees

What fees, spreads, lockups, or transfer limits reduce returns?

Transparency

Are audits, holdings, reserve reports, and administrator updates visible?
