Trending literacy

Trending content should not reward hype

A trending system should show what readers are using while keeping editorial judgment and investor safety intact.

Explain why it trends

Trending can mean timely, useful, or unusually searched.

Example: CPI page trends near release date.

  • Mistake: equating trending with important.
Balance timely and evergreen

Pair popular news with durable guides.

Example: Fed headline links to rate calendar guide.

  • Mistake: sending users into hype loops.
Use trend windows

Daily, weekly, and monthly trending lists serve different jobs.

Example: daily for news, monthly for evergreen resources.

  • Mistake: one undifferentiated ranking.
