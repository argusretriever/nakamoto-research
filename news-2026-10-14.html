ONS reports progress on economic surveys and statistics

Published 12 November 2026

Retrospective archive slot 14 October 2026

The quarterly update covers work on economic statistics and surveys from July to September. It discusses exploratory labour survey reweighting using payroll records and a component approach to understanding productivity.

How to use the release

When comparing employment estimates, record which weights each series uses. Check whether a result belongs to an exploratory analysis or the published headline series before quoting it.
