ONS supply-and-use release separates industry output from value added

Retrospective archive slot: 2026-11-02. Written 3 November 2026. Published 3 November 2026.

Original source: Office for National Statistics. 30 October 2026. Verified 3 November 2026.

What the source establishes

The ONS supply-and-use dataset was released on 30 October. It covers output, consumption, gross value added, imports and exports by product or industry. The newest listed edition spans 1997–2024. ONS labels this dataset Official Statistics in Development.

What it does not establish

A 2026 release date is not a 2026 observation. Output and gross value added are distinct measures; the release listing alone does not establish which sector grew fastest.
