--- PAGE 2 / STREAM 2 ---
Bitcoin Encyclopedia Taxonomy V4 - Master Blueprint
Page 2
How to Use This Blueprint
Each subcategory can become a pillar page, cluster page, article, guide, glossary entry, calculator page,
comparison page, or research report. The repeated article angles are intentional: they help turn one research
topic into multiple useful website assets without relying on filler.
Recommended build order:
- Start with Foundations, Protocol Basics, Wallets, Security, Economics, and Beginner Guides.
- Then add Mining, Lightning, Markets, ETFs, Regulation, and On-Chain Analytics.
- Then build advanced sections: Script, Miniscript, Taproot, Covenants, Chain Analysis, Corporate Treasury, and
Geopolitics.
- Finally add tools: calculators, glossaries, dashboards, comparison pages, and research databases.
