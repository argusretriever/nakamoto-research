Editorial Rule

Every desk must answer the same question.

What changed, who verified it, what can break, and which evergreen guide helps the reader understand it?

Source-first

Primary documents, official dashboards, and reputable reporting are favored over repeated wrappers.

Reader path

Daily news links into guides, tools, sources, and archives so discovery compounds.

Long-term value

Each desk builds a durable research library, not only a stream of daily headlines.
