# Nakamoto Research Growth Instructions

Primary objective: grow Nakamoto Research from hundreds of visitors to 1,000,000+ unique visitors through organic, sustainable growth.

## North Star

Every meaningful change should help at least one durable audience metric:

- Increase unique visitors.
- Increase returning visitors.
- Increase page views and pages per session.
- Increase session duration.
- Reduce bounce rate.
- Improve search visibility, indexing, and rankings.
- Improve reader trust, usefulness, and repeat habit.

## Standing Growth Priorities

- Optimize for traffic growth, user acquisition, unique visitors, returning visitors, page views, pages per session, and session duration.
- Reduce bounce rate by making every page easy to understand, internally linked, and useful within the first screen.
- Improve SEO, search rankings, crawlability, indexability, structured data, metadata, headline quality, and page organization.
- Improve internal linking, content discovery, article recommendations, homepage discoverability, category discoverability, and evergreen content surfacing.
- Surface trending content, popular content, related content, and durable evergreen content without hiding source quality.
- Encourage repeat visits, bookmarking, sharing, retention, engagement, and organic word-of-mouth growth.
- Improve mobile performance, page speed, navigation clarity, and reader flow.
- Maximize organic and long-term audience growth through quality, usefulness, and trust.

## Impact Ranking

When choosing what to fix first, prioritize in this order:

1. Crawlability, indexability, deploy health, sitemap, robots, canonical URLs, and broken-link recovery.
2. Homepage and category discoverability for today's most important stories.
3. Article quality, useful headlines, metadata, structured data, and source clarity.
4. Internal links, related articles, evergreen explainers, and next-read paths.
5. Mobile UX, speed, readability, and layout stability.
6. Returning-visitor loops such as daily briefs, archives, newsletters, saved resources, and recurring watchlists.
7. Sharing and social previews that are accurate, useful, and non-clickbait.

## Non-Negotiables

- Avoid spam tactics.
- Avoid black-hat SEO.
- Avoid clickbait.
- Avoid misleading content.
- Do not invent news, dates, sources, metrics, prices, rankings, or claims.
- Do not trade quality for volume.
- Keep financial content educational, sourced, and clearly framed.
- Do not mislead readers for clicks.
- Do not publish thin pages that exist only to target keywords.
- Do not sacrifice site speed or mobile clarity for decoration.

## Daily Publishing Standard

Every daily update should improve at least one of these growth loops:

- Discovery: better homepage entry points, archive links, category links, metadata, and search-friendly titles.
- Depth: related links, next-read modules, evergreen explainers, and internal paths that increase pages per session.
- Retention: daily brief, archive continuity, recurring watchlists, reader questions, glossary links, and practical checklists.
- Trust: source links, risk labels, dated archives, repeat-story controls, and plain-language caveats.
- Sharing: clear headlines, concise summaries, useful reader takeaways, and non-clickbait social-friendly angles.

For news updates, prefer fewer strong, verified, well-linked stories over many weak items. If adding volume, each item still needs a clear reason to exist: what happened, why it matters, who is affected, and where the reader can go next.

## Page-Level Checklist

Before committing a live update, check:

- Does the homepage surface today's strongest reason to click?
- Does the news page link to archive, RWA, data, markets, security, and evergreen explainers?
- Does the archive preserve old news and make the newest brief easy to find?
- Do headlines explain the value without exaggeration?
- Do source links remain visible?
- Are related pages and next reads obvious?
- Is the page mobile-friendly and readable?
- Are stale dates, repeated nav labels, duplicate sections, and weak filler removed?
- Does the update help a new visitor and a returning visitor?

## Product Rule

Build Nakamoto Research as a long-term crypto and Bitcoin knowledge product, not a disposable news feed. Daily news should connect back to durable explainers, tools, archives, source pages, and reader workflows so each update compounds the value of the whole site.

## Growth Philosophy

Nakamoto Research should win by being useful every day: verified news, plain explanations, strong internal links, durable evergreen pages, reader safety, and clean archives. Growth should come from trust and repeat usefulness, not hype.
